## 2. Summary and Context

The Harlem Youth Mentorship Workshop is committed to continually improving its instructional materials through an ongoing process of review and collaboration. This iterative approach, rooted in evidence-based practices, ensures each update genuinely reflects the needs and aspirations of our mentees and the broader Harlem community we serve.

The slide revision session held on June 23, 2024, marks a key step in our quality assurance efforts. Our primary goals for this session have been to integrate feedback from recent mentees, respond to insights from peer mentors and faculty advisors, and address specific inclusivity and accessibility requests raised by our community partners. By aligning with these priorities, we aim to promote educational rigor, celebrate the lived experience of Harlem’s youth, and deliver mentorship that is both participatory and equitable.

Every change logged in this document is linked to concrete feedback and evaluated for its impact. In particular, revisions have focused on:

- Amplifying authentic youth perspectives through direct mentee input.
- Ensuring learning objectives and workshop activities are both clear and engaging.
- Updating slide visuals and instructions so they are accessible and reflective of Harlem’s dynamic youth community.
- Rigorously documenting all steps for transparency and future reflection.

While our goal is completeness, a technical issue led to the loss of several in-line contributor comments (see Section 4). We have established recovery protocols, and this log provides a detailed map of all changes, supporting our ongoing commitment to collective learning and accountability.

## 4. Technical Issues: Google Drive Syncing Problem

### Overview  
On the morning of June 23, a synchronization issue with Google Drive resulted in the loss of several valuable slide comments, most notably feedback from mentee Luis Martinez. This disruption temporarily halted revision work and necessitated the development of new backup protocols.

### Incident Timeline

- **08:30 AM:** Team accessed Google Drive to begin collaborative review.
- **09:10 AM:** Noticed missing comments (Luis Martinez’ feedback among them).
- **09:25 AM:** Attempted refreshes and troubleshooting without recovery.
- **10:00 AM:** IT support confirmed a Google Drive syncing issue related to Wi-Fi instability.
- **11:15 AM:** Use of version history restored some content, but not missing comments.
- **11:30 AM:** Issue was formally recorded; revision tracker updated to note data loss.
- **1:00 PM:** Revised team procedures to mitigate any future recurrence.

### Technical Diagnosis  
The cause was a disrupted Wi-Fi connection between 8:00 and 9:00 AM, which prevented in-line comments from being saved to the server. Comments made during this window have unfortunately proved unrecoverable.

### Troubleshooting and Mitigation  
- All team members refreshed their browsers and ensured they were logged in.
- Document history was checked; content, but not comments, was recoverable.
- IT recommended immediate adoption of backup protocols, including document exports after feedback cycles.
- A new process was put in place to regularly export comments and maintain offline backups for all collaborative materials.

### Lessons Learned  
Losing specific, context-rich feedback—especially from mentees deeply engaged in the process—represents a real setback for participatory program evaluation. This has highlighted the need for robust redundancy in our knowledge management: routine exports, alternative feedback channels, and clear documentation when issues arise. The team is reaching out to affected contributors and will offer support to ensure feedback is recovered, not just for completeness but for the integrity of the mentoring experience.
