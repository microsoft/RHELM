## Reflections: Challenges & Lessons Learned

### Technical Challenges
- **Projector sync failure**: The MacBook slides failed to display correctly, forcing a pivot to printed handouts. This broke my presentation rhythm and reduced visual impact.
- **Lesson**: Always test AV setup in the actual venue beforehand. Carry backup formats (PDF on USB, printed copies) as standard practice, not just as emergency fallback.

### Social Challenges
- **Solo facilitation**: Dr. Grant's late arrival left me fielding difficult questions without collaborative support. The roundtable format depends on multiple voices; one person cannot carry it alone.
- **Dinner environment**: The cramped, noisy restaurant made genuine conversation nearly impossible. Large group dinners at unfamiliar venues are high-risk for meaningful connection.
- **Lesson**: Establish a contingency plan for co-facilitator absences. For future group dinners, scout venues in advance or suggest smaller breakout groups.

### Emotional Challenges
- **Methodology criticism**: Dr. Nair's critique about insufficient community input struck a nerve. While valid, receiving it publicly and while physically unwell amplified the emotional weight.
- **Self-doubt**: By evening, I found myself questioning the project's trajectory and my own effectiveness. The accumulation of setbacks—technical, social, physical—eroded my confidence.
- **Lesson**: Separate feedback content from delivery context. Schedule a calm follow-up review of critiques rather than internalizing them in the moment. Physical wellness directly affects emotional resilience; prioritize allergy management before high-stakes days.
