## Planning Notes, Substitutions, and Reminders

- **Allergen Awareness:** I always read labels at the shelf, even for products I buy regularly. Manufacturing processes and recipes change unexpectedly, and student health is my top priority.
- **Substitutions:** If SunButter is missing, I choose soy butter or another approved spread, ensuring it’s truly nut-free. For greens, I’ll swap spinach or kale with other seasonal offerings like chard or lettuce if needed.
- **Flexibility:** I adjust portions depending on the number of children, staff, or meal planning needs that week. If pre-cut carrots are sold out, I prep them myself—sometimes together with students as part of practical life activities.
- **Montessori Materials:** Alberta Co-op doesn’t stock specific Montessori materials, but I regularly use food items for hands-on classroom lessons in sorting, slicing, measuring, and sharing.
- **Produce Seasonality:** Local fruit and vegetable options vary daily depending on farm deliveries. I check what’s freshest and adapt lesson plans accordingly.
- **Sustainability:** I prioritize products with compostable packaging and buy in bulk when practical. These choices help teach children about stewardship and respect for the environment.
