## Daily Progress and Meeting Summaries

### March 1 (Friday)
I integrated findings from recent meta-analyses on school-based nutrition interventions into the systematic review draft, drawing especially on a notable article published in *The Lancet Public Health*. Using the most current cohort data, I cleaned the pilot database and flagged outliers in self-reported protein intake, paying particular attention to entries outside typical physiological ranges. I also connected with Dr. Alonso to resolve several budget allocation clarifications for the EU grant proposal, ensuring our application aligns fully with funding agency requirements.

### March 2 (Saturday)
During the morning literature review, I identified two influential interventions described by Mozaffarian et al. (2023) that address micronutrient supplementation for older adults. Later, in a productive online meeting with Dr. Sánchez, we confirmed methodological agreements for the adolescent obesity review, which solidifies the study’s analytical rigor. In preparation for next week, I coordinated a Zoom test to ensure the upcoming cross-disciplinary team meeting will run smoothly.

### March 3 (Sunday)
I reviewed three PhD research proposals this afternoon. Two demonstrated a strong commitment to public health equity—an encouraging trend—while one required additional guidance and was flagged for significant revision. Meanwhile, I began drafting the university newsletter update, highlighting our recent publication in *Nutrients* and showcasing the team's efforts over the quarter.

### March 4 (Monday)
The full draft of the systematic review was completed and circulated among collaborators for consensus synthesis and peer editing. I also updated the project dashboard, marking key data collection milestones and cataloging our recent manuscript submissions and conference abstracts.

### March 5 (Tuesday)
After running preliminary statistical models on the Mediterranean Diet pilot cohort, I observed a significant positive association between fiber intake and reduced C-reactive protein (CRP) levels, which may reinforce the anti-inflammatory benefits of the diet. I sent the first version of the public newsletter update to our Communications Officer and included a graphical preview illustrating trends in dietary fiber intake across age groups.

### March 6 (Wednesday)
The EU grant application draft was submitted to Dr. Müller for a final review. I continued with the weekly literature review, documenting the three most recent WHO guidelines (2023) on micronutrient intake for older populations. New findings were integrated into our comparative table on micronutrient status in older adults, providing the team with an updated reference for ongoing and future analyses.

### March 7 (Thursday)
I moderated a successful cross-disciplinary team meeting, emphasizing key findings from the recent literature on the 'double burden of malnutrition' and its growing relevance in both local and international contexts. The meeting fostered valuable discussion and new collaborative opportunities. Finally, I completed the weekly update of our project outputs in the institutional repository and formally logged the week’s achievements—along with emerging challenges such as inconsistencies in self-reported protein data, which will need targeted methodological refinement in the coming month.
