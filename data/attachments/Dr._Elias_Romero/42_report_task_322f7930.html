Detailed Discussion and Outcomes

1. Horizon Europe Grant Proposal (Dr. Marta Alvarez)

  • Context and Significance:
    The proposed project seeks to address health inequalities in urban Barcelona by examining dietary patterns and piloting community-based interventions. Dr. Alvarez emphasized that the grant directly corresponds with Horizon Europe’s priority on reducing health disparities and creating healthier cities. Particular focus will be given to how the findings can inform both municipal nutritional policies and broader public health practice.

    To maximize the proposal's impact and competitiveness, the group agreed to solidify partnerships with local public health institutes, community organizations, and selected international universities. Dr. Alvarez suggested expanding the methodological section to reflect multidisciplinary inputs.

    Decisions and Next Steps:
    - The team will refine objectives, update the consortium member list, and clarify partners’ participation.
    - Budget planning will proceed in parallel with partner confirmation, with an internal deadline set for September 18.

2. Integration of Urban Art Photographs (Dr. Elias Romero)

  • Rationale and Impact:
    Dr. Romero articulated how urban art represents a powerful vehicle for illustrating the social and cultural dimensions of nutrition in urban spaces. Bringing these visuals into research communication is intended to foster greater connection with both academic audiences and the general public.

    Drawing on recent research into public art's role in community engagement, the proposal involves piloting the placement of selected photographic vignettes alongside the project’s narrative content. The visuals are expected to make complex epidemiological findings more accessible and relatable while amplifying key health promotion messages.

    After reviewing several candidate photographs and discussing technical requirements, the team unanimously endorsed the idea and agreed to assemble an initial set of images for review by September 23.

3. Project Management and Implementation

  • Action Items and Assignment:
    Deliverables and responsibilities were distributed for each team member, with emphasis on transparency and collaboration:

    • Dr. Alvarez will finalize the research design, lead partner coordination, and prepare budget materials.
    • Dr. Romero will curate relevant urban art photos, develop a strategy for integrating visuals, and manage communication regarding image usage with the university’s ethics and communications offices.
    • Anna Torres will provide a detailed literature review on intersections between urban art, public engagement, and nutrition outcomes, as well as compile case study examples.
    • Luis Ferrer will structure the project timeline, establish a central documentation system for all materials, and oversee workflow monitoring.

    Each task is supported by a clear deadline and integrated into a shared project tracker for accountability.
