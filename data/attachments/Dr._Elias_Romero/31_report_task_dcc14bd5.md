## 2. Agenda Overview

The meeting opened with a confirmation of both participants’ presence and a brief recap of action points from the previous session. The main focus was to review the progress of ongoing project deliverables, discuss recent developments in literature and data curation, and evaluate the integrative analysis pipeline.

### Project Progress Update

Dr. Romero and Dr. Dubois provided updates on their respective work package deliverables. Dr. Romero summarized the latest efforts in dataset standardization and noted successful completion of preliminary data validation, referencing protocols established in the project charter. Dr. Dubois reported on the literature review process, highlighting several recent publications relevant to their modeling approach, which were circulated prior to the meeting.

### Research Discussion – Deferred

A substantive discussion of the integrative analysis pipeline was planned for this meeting. However, both participants encountered persistent technical difficulties, including unstable connections and the inability to reliably share and collaboratively edit documents via screen sharing. These issues significantly limited their ability to review key research materials in real time and exchange annotated feedback. Given the importance of this session for upcoming project milestones, they jointly decided to postpone the detailed review. Both agreed to focus the next meeting exclusively on this topic, with assurances that all technical issues will be addressed in advance.

### Technical Troubleshooting

A considerable portion of the meeting was dedicated to diagnosing and resolving the ongoing technical difficulties. These challenges not only hindered productive discussion but also raised concerns about the reliability of the videoconferencing platform for future sessions. The participants systematically documented each problem and solution attempt (see Section 3 for details), and agreed to escalate the matter to their respective institutional IT departments.

### Administrative Matters

Outstanding administrative items were reviewed, with clear assignments given for technical follow-up and core project tasks. Points of clarification included compliance with institutional data sharing policies and deadlines for circulating preparatory materials before the next session.
