3. Progress Update and Strategic Adjustments

Throughout July, the research team has made several important adjustments in response to challenges and early feedback. As discussed in the July 20 meeting, recruitment for the study fell short of projections. Initial reliance on passive digital platforms did not reach or sufficiently engage our intended participants. To address this, the team will now focus on forging partnerships with community health centers and local advocacy groups. This approach aims to connect with potential participants more directly and meaningfully, and should help accelerate progress toward recruitment goals.

The pilot phase of the digital dietary assessment tool provided useful insights for further refinement. The tool’s interface was well received in initial interactions; however, dropout rates increased notably at the survey’s midpoint. Participants indicated that onboarding guidance and reminders would help them remain engaged. The team is therefore implementing a more structured onboarding sequence and adding scheduled check-in points by email or phone for participants. The data collection timeline has been extended by a week to allow these improvements to take effect and to support more robust baseline data gathering.

Interdepartmental collaboration emerged as a key focus area during the latest meeting. Effective coordination with IT and statistics teams is essential, both to resolve technical bottlenecks in the dietary tool and to ensure a secure and tidy data pipeline for subsequent analysis. The absence of sufficient baseline data has required a shift in analytical emphasis for the upcoming interim report—there will be greater focus on process evaluation and qualitative findings, as these provide immediate insight into recruitment, engagement, and tool usability.

4. Next Steps and Planned Actions

A. Grant Reporting - The interim project report for the funding agency will be finalized and submitted by July 31. This report will candidly address recruitment delays, describe the revised outreach strategy, and present early feedback on the digital dietary tool, alongside updated project metrics. - The project’s internal Gantt chart and deliverables summary will be revised to capture the true pace of work and adjustments, ensuring accurate and transparent reporting for the grantor.

B. Ethics and Protocols - An amendment to the project’s ethics protocol will be prepared and submitted, accounting for changes in recruitment plans and digital engagement strategies. - The team is also drafting a protocol addendum to formalize the new participant onboarding and retention measures. This document will be distributed to all team members and submitted for expedited ethics review to minimize project disruption.

C. Interdepartmental Coordination - Meetings with IT and statistics specialists are confirmed for July 29 and will recur as needed. These sessions will focus on technical troubleshooting, refining analytics, and ensuring a seamless data workflow. - A bi-weekly reporting cycle with department leads is being established to foster clear communication regarding progress, obstacles, and methodological changes.
