## 3. Preparation & Cooking Process

### A. Preparation Steps

1. **Handwashing and Sanitation:**  
   I always begin with a thorough handwash and sanitize all kitchen surfaces and utensils. This step is fundamental to prevent any risk of foodborne illness (see [3]).

2. **Weighing and Rinsing Lentils:**  
   Weighed out 500g of dry lentils, then rinsed them under cold water to remove dust and possible residues. This step also helps discard some anti-nutrients, such as phytates, improving mineral uptake (see [3]).

3. **Chopping Fresh Vegetables:**  
   I chopped the onions, carrots, celery, bell pepper, tomato, chard, and parsley just before cooking. Working fast at this stage retains more vitamins and phytonutrients, as they degrade quickly once cut (see [4]).

4. **Mise en Place:**  
   Assembling measured spices, oil, salt, and water is my technique for keeping cooking organized. It’s also critical for accurately tracking nutrients, especially in research contexts.

### B. Cooking Steps

1. **Initial Sauté:**  
   I started by sautéing the onions, carrots, and celery in olive oil over medium heat for six minutes. This softens their structure, makes them more digestible, and boosts the absorption of carotenoids since olive oil acts as a carrier (see [4]).

2. **Building Flavor:**  
   Added garlic, bell pepper, and smoked paprika, then continued sautéing for two minutes. This short, gentle heat helps preserve sensitive vitamins and aromatic compounds (see [5]), adding depth with the paprika’s smoky quality.

3. **Incorporating Tomatoes:**  
   Once veggies softened, I stirred in chopped tomato and cooked for five minutes until most liquid evaporated. Heat here improves lycopene bioavailability and gives a richer flavor base.

4. **Combining Main Ingredients:**  
   Lentils, chard, water, bay leaf, and salt went into the pot next. I mixed everything thoroughly to ensure even seasoning and flavor penetration.

5. **Simmering:**  
   Brought the stew to a boil, then turned the heat to low and simmered with the lid on for 45 minutes. Slow simmering hydrates the lentils evenly and protects delicate B vitamins from excessive heat loss (see [5]).

6. **Finishing Touches:**  
   For the last ten minutes, I stirred in parsley and black pepper. Adding parsley late keeps its vitamin C and freshness intact.

7. **Cooling and Food Safety:**  
   After seasoning to taste and removing the bay leaf, I rapidly cooled the stew from 60°C down to under 5°C within two hours. This cooling window is essential for preventing bacterial growth, especially in larger batches (see [3]).

## 4. Sensory Assessment & Adjustments

### Sensory Evaluation (11:00)

Just before lunch, I tasted a ladleful straight from the pot:

- **Aroma:** Strong smoky scents from paprika mingled with the earthy notes of lentils and fresh garlic.  
- **Taste:** Well-balanced, with the sweet carrots and soft onions offsetting the robust green flavors from the chard.  
- **Texture:** Lentils were perfectly tender, holding their shape; the vegetables were soft but not overcooked, and the chard added a bright contrast.  
- **Color:** A vibrant, deep red-brown, flecked with greens and reds from the chard, peppers, and parsley.

### Immediate Adjustments

The initial taste felt slightly undersalted, so I stirred in an additional 2g of sea salt and another 3g chopped parsley. After the adjustment, the stew tasted lively and well-rounded, with no need to further tweak the paprika.

### Dietary Implications

By combining lentils and a medley of vegetables, the stew packs a punch:  
- **Fiber-rich**: Promotes digestive health and may lower cholesterol.  
- **Micronutrient-dense**: Provides generous amounts of iron, potassium, folate, and vitamins A and C—nutrients often under-consumed, especially in local Mediterranean populations ([4]).  
- **Healthy fat profile**: Uses only extra virgin olive oil, minimizing saturated fats typical in less traditional stews, in keeping with regional nutrition guidelines ([5]).

Choosing local, fresh produce not only enhances flavor but also supports sustainable food practices and strengthens the connection to Barcelona’s culinary traditions.
