## 4. Reflection: Disruptions & Mood

### Morning Assessment
The day started with genuine intention: a quiet yoga session on the balcony, followed by a solitary hike through one of Barcelona's most characterful neighborhoods. Neither unfolded as planned.

### Key Disruptions
1. **Construction noise** — completely undermined the balcony yoga/meditation session. The inability to control sound in an outdoor space remains a recurring frustration.
2. **Nina incident** — though minor, the spilled tea and cleanup disrupted the transition from meditation to movement. The startle response in Nina is a reminder to keep fragile items away from the balcony edge.
3. **Festival setup in Gràcia** — transformed a familiar, quiet walking route into a loud, crowded obstacle course. The detours were physically manageable but mentally draining; the purpose of the hike (quiet reflection, solitude) was entirely defeated.
4. **Market shortages** — the unavailability of eggplant and tomatoes directly impacts the batch cooking plan for the week. Substitutions will need to be improvised.
5. **Broken bag** — a small but demoralizing incident. Produce scattered on the pavement attracted unwanted attention and added to the sense of a day going wrong at every turn.

### Mood Trajectory

| Time | Mood | Trigger |
|------|------|---------|
| 6:30 a.m. | Calm, optimistic | Beginning yoga session |
| 6:45 a.m. | Frustrated, agitated | Construction noise + Nina knocking over tea |
| 7:15 a.m. | Determined, slightly tense | Setting out for hike; hoping to reset |
| 8:00 a.m. | Irritated, overstimulated | Festival crowds and forced detours |
| 9:00 a.m. | Disappointed, deflated | Market shortages + broken bag incident |
| Midday onward | Drained, headache onset | Cumulative effect of repeated setbacks |

### Closing Thought
Not every day cooperates with good intentions. The morning routine—usually a source of grounding—became a series of small failures. Tomorrow: try again. Move the yoga session indoors if construction persists. Check festival schedules before planning outdoor routes. Replace the broken shopping bag.
