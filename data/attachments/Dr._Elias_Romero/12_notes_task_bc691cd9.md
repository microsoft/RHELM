Today’s total steps—over 13,000—not only exceeded my usual goal, but they also surpassed the commonly recommended 10,000 steps per day set by organizations like the CDC and WHO. According to research, consistently reaching or exceeding 12,000 steps can mean even greater health advantages, especially for city dwellers who walk regularly [3].

- The CDC suggests most adults should aim for 7,000–10,000 steps a day, equivalent to 30+ minutes of moderate activity.
- WHO recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity or 75 minutes of vigorous-intensity activity weekly.
