## Departmental Updates and Digital Notes

**Methodological Improvements**

- We have standardized dietary intake data analysis software (moving departmental teams to a unified platform), which should enhance data comparability and streamline training for incoming staff.
- The revised biomarker validation protocol is now in effect. This protocol incorporates rigorous new quality checks, particularly for blood-based nutrient assays, and aligns with international best practice recommendations.
- Our dietary intake analyses are now fully harmonized using the EuroFIR nutrient database, supporting cross-country comparisons within our European Union collaborations.

**Collaborative Workflows and Infrastructure**

- The DataVerse platform pilot for shared, secure data management is underway. Initial feedback from project leads has been positive, indicating improved efficiency in multi-center data aggregation and cleaning.
- Lead and co-investigator responsibilities for ongoing EU-funded nutritional intervention projects have been clarified. Role descriptions are available via the secure department portal for reference.
- Monthly cross-departmental methodological review meetings are now institutionalized. These sessions are intended to support quality assurance, problem-solving, and continuous methodological refinement.

**Key Evidence-Based Decisions and Requests for Input**

- We will discuss the integration of novel biomarker endpoints (e.g., metabolomics, emerging micronutrient markers) in the next phase of our cohort studies. Input from laboratory and epidemiology specialists is essential to finalize analytic priorities.
- Standardizing socioeconomic status indices remains a priority for comparability across future Spanish cohort studies. Please review candidate indices (INE, MEDEA, ESeC) ahead of the meeting for a vote.
- I am proposing a pilot project for digital food records with select participant groups. We need to deliberate on appropriate feasibility metrics, user support resources, and privacy implications ahead of the rollout.

## Action Items for Meeting

- **Review and Prioritize Recent Peer-Reviewed Publications**  
  All project leads will present newly identified articles relevant for current and upcoming funding cycles. This collaborative review will help us identify research that supports active grant applications and guides new study protocols.
  
- **Methodological Standardization Across Projects**  
  Data teams and principal investigators will report on their progress with unified dietary intake and nutrient analysis tools. Please highlight any technical hurdles, training requirements, or compatibility issues encountered during the transition.
  
- **Discussion on Digital Food Record Pilot**  
  The digital workflow group will outline the scope and design of the proposed digital food record pilot. We need to agree on participant selection criteria, data collection logistics, and the approach to validating digital entries against traditional methods.
  
- **Biomarker Endpoint Selection**  
  Our laboratory and epidemiology leads, in conjunction with ethical advisors, will develop consensus recommendations for biomarker selection. Discussion will cover technical feasibility, cost considerations, and participant consent.
  
- **Socioeconomic Status Index Selection**  
  Social epidemiology and harmonization teams will finalize the choice of a standardized socioeconomic status metric. The selected tool should maximize comparability within Spain and facilitate pooled analyses with our European partners.
  
- **Nominations for EU Nutrition Policy Working Groups**  
  We will nominate departmental delegates for the next round of EU nutrition policy forums and working groups. Interested candidates should review the responsibilities ahead of the meeting.
  
- **Agenda for Next Cross-Departmental Methods Review**  
  All methodological leads are invited to propose topics for discussion at our next scheduled methods review, ensuring the agenda reflects emerging needs and shared priorities.
