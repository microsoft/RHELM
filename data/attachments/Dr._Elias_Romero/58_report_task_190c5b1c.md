## 3. Meeting Highlights

### A. Project and Administrative Updates

- The team has now completed approximately 80% of preliminary data cleaning for the Mediterranean dietary patterns study. This milestone puts the project on track for the targeted data freeze next week.
- Dr. Nowak presented initial findings from the nutrient intake sub-cohort, focusing on adolescent participants. Discussion centered on incomplete covariate data, particularly concerns about bias in self-reported dietary intake within specific subgroups.
- Dr. Romero emphasized the importance of linking this project’s findings to broader efforts under the LifeCourse Nutritional Interventions initiative, aiming for a more cohesive approach to nutritional surveillance and analyses.
- Administrative reminders included a checkpoint for the “EpiNutri-Youth” grant submission, particularly ensuring that all co-investigator sections are compiled, and the pre-submission checklist is up to date. Dr. Keller will coordinate with contributing authors as part of this process.
- Data sharing and harmonization procedures for LifeCourse collaboration remain a key priority. Dr. Keller is to finalize data transfer timeframes once platform access issues are resolved.

### B. Technical Issues

- **Audio Instability (12:25–12:32 PM):**  
  During the discussion of statistical methods, Dr. Keller’s intermittent audio loss caused certain clarifying questions from Dr. Nowak to go unanswered. Some points concerning subgroup definitions and data reconciliation remain unresolved and will require follow-up by email.
- **Microphone Malfunction (12:56–12:59 PM):**  
  Dr. Nowak’s microphone failed briefly while he was detailing imputation strategies for missing dietary data. As his explanations were neither recorded nor discussed in real time, he is to provide a written summary for the project drive.

### C. Impact Assessment

- The technical difficulties delayed key decisions on the imputation strategy for missing data and the approach to participant subgroup stratification. A few action items therefore require further clarification outside this meeting.
- While Dr. Romero confirmed all administrative and submission deadlines, the group was unable to confirm data transfer dates due to unresolved issues with platform reliability and incomplete feedback.
