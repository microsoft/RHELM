## 2. Activities and Methodology

### System Diagnostics and Stability

We began the session with a thorough system health check. Standard diagnostics were run via Windows Security, Dell SupportAssist, and by manually reviewing system logs. The examination focused particularly on detecting recent crashes, hardware alerts, or system anomalies, but found overall stability: no malfunctions or signs of instability were present. We confirmed that all critical firmware and Windows updates were installed as of September 28, 2024, ensuring the device was current with manufacturer and security recommendations.

### Secure Backup Process

Security of data—especially legal documents—remained our main concern throughout the backup operation. In alignment with Bar Council of India and IT Act requirements [1][2], strict confidentiality and traceability protocols were enforced in all handling and movement of files.

- **Backup Software and Encryption:**  
    We utilized Acronis True Image for both complete system backups and individual file-level copies [4]. For external media, we applied Veracrypt encryption [5], while internal storage remained protected with BitLocker encryption [6]. Dual encryption acted as an additional safeguard against unauthorized access.

- **Audit Logging:**  
    All backup activity was logged through the backup software and corroborated by manual records. Digital logs were configured with tamper-evident features to assure future auditability.

### Security and Confidentiality

Only Justice Mehra and Mr. Sharma had direct access to the laptop, backup media, and storage locations. Backup drives were individually labeled, cataloged, and physically stored in secured, access-controlled legal archives. Cloud backups were transmitted to Acronis Cloud servers located in India, configured to ISO/IEC 27001 standards [3][4]. All files containing legal data were encrypted prior to transfer, maintaining compliance with both local and international regulations.

### Data Verification and Validation

After each backup, we checked data integrity by comparing original files and backup copies using SHA256 hashes. Random samples of legal documents were opened, tested for accessibility, and compared for consistency. Backup logs, including timestamp verification and operation details, were exported, independently secured, and digitally archived with tamper-resistance controls.

## 4. System Health Review and Future Recommendations

### Observations

Diagnostics revealed no hardware failure, disk errors, or operating system instability. The firmware and operating system were both up-to-date. The SSD showed 99% lifespan remaining with a record of 2,300 power-on hours, indicating healthy wear-leveling and reliable ongoing performance.

### Identified Risks

No major vulnerabilities emerged during the analysis. However, continued attention must be paid to proper password management. Notably, the risk of unauthorized access grows if encryption passwords are not changed regularly. At the time of review, all active user accounts were protected by multi-factor authentication, and no dormant or legacy accounts were present.

### Recommendations

#### Data Security

- Maintain layered encryption for both hardware and software levels across all sensitive legal files [5][6].
- Rotate system and encryption passwords at regular intervals, ideally every quarter.
- Audit user account access each month and immediately disable any inactive accounts.
- Preserve all backup activity logs securely in tamper-evident archives, and arrange periodic, independent audits [3].

#### Backup Procedures

- Schedule biweekly backups for legal documents and monthly backups for personal files.
- Test backup restoration periodically to ensure both reliability and completeness.
- Update backup and data handling protocols whenever regulations or technology standards change.

#### Physical and Network Security

- Store backup drives in premises with double-lock and controlled access authorization; maintain physical security logs.
- Limit cloud storage to India-based, ISO/IEC 27001-certified data centers [3][4].
- Regularly review and update firewall, antivirus, and endpoint protection policies throughout the year.

#### Legal and Regulatory Compliance

- Continue strict adherence to the Information Technology Act, 2000 and all relevant Bar Council guidelines [1][2].
- Archive digital signatures and full audit logs for at least seven years, ensuring availability for any legal or compliance review.
