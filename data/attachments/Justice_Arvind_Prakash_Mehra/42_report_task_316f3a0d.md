## Executive Summary

This report provides a detailed account of electricity and water usage at my residence in New Delhi for the month of August 2024. Compared to July, both utilities show a clear downward trend, with electricity usage falling by 7% and water consumption decreasing by 9%. These improvements are directly linked to targeted conservation efforts undertaken after reviewing previous consumption patterns and identifying areas for optimization. Strategies included refining air conditioning schedules, switching to LED lighting, installing water-saving devices, and encouraging conscious usage among household staff.

The financial aspect aligns with my commitment to responsible and transparent household management. I have used verified billing data and digital transaction records sourced from BSES Yamuna Power Ltd and the Delhi Jal Board. All expenses and actions conform with constitutional principles relating to public accountability and sustainability, ensuring readiness for any internal or external audit.

## III. Observations and Key Actions

### Utility Consumption Patterns

Over the past two months, a consistent decline in both electricity and water consumption has been recorded. Much of this progress stems from prioritizing efficiency in daily routines, such as adjusting air conditioner settings and being more mindful of water use across the home.

### Conservation Measures

- **Energy Management:**  
  From early August, I adopted stricter air conditioning schedules, particularly by reducing usage during evening hours. The transition to LED lights throughout the residence was completed as planned. To further control power usage, household appliances now operate according to fixed timings.
- **Water Savings:**  
  I oversaw the installation of flow restrictors on taps in the kitchen and washrooms. Garden watering was scaled back to essential intervals only. I also led training sessions with household staff, emphasizing the importance of every conservation measure.
  
### Governance and Record-Keeping

Every step—from payment to documentation—adheres to judicial standards of fiscal discipline and transparency. All receipts, bills, and payment confirmations are securely stored and can be accessed for review at any time. This systematic approach ensures the household remains audit-ready and upholds best practices for financial governance.

## IV. Recommendations for Continued Progress

To maintain and build on these improvements, I plan the following actions:

- Host monthly meetings with staff to discuss consumption data and review ongoing practices.
- Continue educating all members of the household about resource conservation and sustainability.
- Conduct periodic audits comparing household consumption against benchmarks established by utility companies and local best practices.

These measures will ensure sustained progress in utility management and strengthen accountability.
