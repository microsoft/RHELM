## Maintenance Activities and Log

During the scheduled session, the following activities were conducted to ensure optimal functioning and security compliance:

- **System Diagnostics:** Hardware health was evaluated, with all performance benchmarks checked. No hardware faults or overheating issues were detected.  
- **Malware and Vulnerability Scan:** A full system scan was performed using McAfee Security Suite, supplemented by Windows Security. The system was cleared of malware, unauthorized installations, and flagged vulnerabilities in line with NIST SP 800-92 requirements.  
- **Software Updates:** All critical operating system and third-party applications were inspected, and necessary updates were installed.  
- **Security Controls Check:** Security features, including disk encryption, login audit trails, and multi-factor authentication, were carefully reviewed. All measures were found to be actively enforced.
- **Data Backup:** A full system backup was taken and validated, supporting business continuity and legal compliance goals.
- **Issue Documentation:** A minor issue was observed during the Dell SupportAssist update. The error was logged, and remediation followed Dell technical guidelines, resolving the problem without data loss or system instability.

These steps collectively strengthened the device’s readiness for secure, reliable operation, closely tracking with best practices and compliance measures.

## Recommendations for Future Maintenance

Regular maintenance is essential to keep IT systems secure, reliable, and compliant. The following recommendations are proposed to support ongoing operational excellence and regulatory adherence:

- **Establish Monthly Maintenance Schedule:** Continue systematic monthly reviews, covering software updates and backup verification, to keep devices audit-ready and compliant.
- **Implement Centralized Patch Management:** Transition to automated patch deployment and centralized logging as recommended by ISO/IEC 27001 and NIST SP 800-92. This will improve traceability and facilitate proactive risk mitigation.
- **Enhance Authentication Controls:** Require quarterly reviews of multi-factor authentication and simulated penetration tests. These procedures help protect constitutional confidentiality and maintain security standards.
- **Adopt Geo-Redundant Backup Storage:** Introduce geo-redundant, encrypted storage for backups to align with judicial data protection mandates and Supreme Court requirements.
- **Maintain Audit-Ready Documentation:** Ensure all maintenance reports use standardized formats (per ISO 9001) for rapid retrieval and supportive use in audits or legal proceedings.
- **Integrate Incident Response Logging:** Build incident response tracking for all anomalies or threats as specified in NIST SP 800-92, strengthening record-keeping and evidentiary support for future judicial review.

Taken together, these steps will help safeguard vital assets and uphold institutional responsibilities in managing sensitive data.
