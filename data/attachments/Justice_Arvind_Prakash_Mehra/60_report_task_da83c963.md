## Technical Issues Encountered

### Description and Investigation of the Syncing Error

On initiating my weekly update, I noticed discrepancies between reconciled figures and live bank statements. OneDrive’s automated alert signaled that some files had reverted to an earlier version. Upon closer inspection, I discovered that recent records, which had not yet been manually exported, were inadvertently overwritten. A review of version history confirmed the error originated from a failed update in the cloud backup protocol.

This technical malfunction resulted in the loss of critical financial evidence for certain entries, impacting my audit trail and posing potential risks for regulatory compliance.

### Methods Used to Identify the Problem

- Alert received from OneDrive regarding file version changes.
- Manual cross-check against bank statements flagged missing supporting files.
- Several receipts and documentation were not found during my regular evidence chain audit.

### Direct Outcomes

- Documentation for last week’s reconciliation was largely lost, as were supporting files for some entries this week.
- Four entries currently lack complete electronic or paper evidence.
- The consistency and completeness required for ICAI compliance, as well as secure audit chains, were interrupted.

### Corrective Actions and Safeguards

To prevent similar occurrences, I am strengthening safeguards as follows:
- Instituting dual maintenance of electronic and printed records, as recommended by ICAI’s Technical Guide on Internal Audit and Supreme Court chain-of-custody standards.
- Establishing weekly checkpoint procedures for data integrity, including review and signature from the reporting authority.
- Setting up offline, tamper-proof backups (PDF/A format) in addition to encrypted cloud storage utilizing two-factor authentication.
- Performing weekly validations of backup processes and immediate documentation of any errors or procedural changes.
- Sharing lessons from this incident with staff, where applicable, and integrating tighter controls into our workflow.
