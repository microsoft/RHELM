## Purpose of Review

This report records and evaluates our household’s financial activities from 5 April to 18 April 2024. ## Executive Summary

Over the past fortnight, I have meticulously tracked every expense and investment-related decision in our household. My primary aim was to ensure that each transaction reflects our shared values of financial discipline and transparency. All expenditures were cross-checked using receipts and bank statements; investment decisions were consciously aligned with our risk comfort and current needs.

I have included Sunita in all discussions—her contributions helped clarify details, challenge assumptions, and strengthen our decisions. Where there were queries or matters that require follow-up, I’ve documented them so nothing gets overlooked.

### Approach

Every entry in this review has been verified with supporting documents and evidence. In line with Indian Accounting Standards (Ind AS), I reviewed our outflows and investments, referenced relevant Supreme Court judgments regarding evidence and prudence, and ensured all decision-making adhered to best practices.

## Consultation and Sunita’s Role: Process and Reflections

Sunita’s involvement throughout this period deserves special mention. She collected and presented most of the household receipts, clarified uncertainties about several entries, and expressed thoughtful concerns—particularly about the vegetable purchase process and the real estate decision.

Some highlights from our discussions:

- **Insurance Premiums:** Sunita requested more details about our upcoming insurance schedules. I’ve noted this, and will bring updated figures to our next review.
- **Vegetable Purchases:** Given the lack of formal receipts at the local market, I made sure to confirm payment and quantity directly with the vendor. Sunita helped keep track of these amounts.
- **Real Estate Investment:** Sunita asked when we might revisit the NCR plot purchase. Together we decided to postpone until July and will review market trends in the interim.

All major decisions were made jointly. The single pending issue remains our insurance premium plan, which I’ll clarify at our meeting next month.
