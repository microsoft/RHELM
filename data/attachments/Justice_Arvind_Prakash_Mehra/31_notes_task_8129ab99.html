Daily Health Log

Date: April 21, 2024
Name: Justice Arvind Prakash Mehra
Designation: Retired Supreme Court Judge, Senior Advocate

1. Morning

Wake-up Time: 5:45 AM

Physical Condition Upon Waking

On waking this morning, I noticed that familiar, mild joint stiffness in my knees and fingers. Fortunately, it wasn’t as pronounced as in previous days, and eased significantly after a few minutes of gentle movement. I managed to get up without assistance and walked to the bathroom unassisted, although I hesitated slightly with my first steps until I had stretched. No issues with balance or any risk of falling.

I woke up without any muscle pain or noticeable fatigue and felt that my sleep had been restful overall. I estimate I got about seven hours of sleep—went to bed around 10:30 PM and woke naturally just before 5:45 AM, though I lingered in bed for fifteen minutes before getting up completely.

Morning Activities

Shortly after refreshing myself, I took my usual walk in the garden from 6:10 to 6:40 AM. The weather was pleasantly cool, and I kept a comfortable pace, pausing occasionally to enjoy the flowers and the birdsong. The walk was light and restorative; I felt no need for a cane or other support.

At 6:45, I completed my prescribed stretching routine—focusing on both upper and lower limbs as my physiotherapist has advised. These gentle stretches help loosen up the joints before breakfast and remain an anchor in my morning.

Around 7:10, I settled in a quiet space, closed my eyes, and followed a guided mindfulness meditation for fifteen minutes. After this session, I felt a tangible sense of calm, alertness, and focus that lingered through the morning.

Unfortunately, I did not take my blood pressure, fasting glucose, pulse oximetry, or weight readings this morning—something I need to be more diligent about in the coming days.

3. Afternoon

Energy Levels

The afternoon brought an encouraging lift in my energy—noticeably better than yesterday. I think one reason was the uninterrupted, restful sleep last night, and another was that lunch was light but filling. Between 1:30 and 5:00 PM, my mind remained alert; I didn’t experience my usual post-lunch lethargy.

Outdoor and Wellness Activities

Around 2:15 PM, I spent about fifteen minutes with our household staff reviewing our kitchen composting setup. Standing and moving as we spoke, I enjoyed discussing ways to better separate biodegradable and non-biodegradable waste. This small outdoor task gave me a sense of purpose and connection to routines beyond my own health.

Later, following my physiotherapist’s plan, I used a resistance band for a set of light upper-limb exercises—just ten minutes, but enough to maintain some tone and flexibility.

Cognitive Engagement

At 3:00, I sat down for a crossword puzzle and afterward thumbed through several legal periodicals. My attention span and recall felt sharp; I solved the puzzle with minimal struggle and found myself fully immersed in my reading.

Physical and Vocal Limitations

Although my voice has been strained lately, by today the hoarseness was milder, and I noticed less discomfort when I read aloud. I believe this is partly due to drinking more water and giving myself longer periods of vocal rest. Physically, I didn’t notice any new complaints or limitations today.

Summary of Improvements

There is a clear improvement in my afternoon alertness and vocal strength. I haven’t noticed any negative changes or regressions.

4. Evening

Leisure and Social Activities

The evening was restorative and enjoyable. At 7:45, I played two games of chess with my grandchild. We laughed and strategized, and I was pleased to keep up my concentration throughout. Afterward, I put on a classical instrumental playlist and simply listened for about twenty minutes, letting the music lift my mood and bring a soothing end to the day.

Between 8:50 and 9:20, I read two essays from a collected volume. The quiet focus carried over from the music. My mind felt both relaxed and content.

Mood Assessment

Using my daily self-check, I rated my evening mood as 4 out of 5—generally positive, with only slight, healthy fatigue at the end of the day. Family interactions felt particularly meaningful tonight; spending that quality time made a noticeable difference in my overall well-being.

Mental and Emotional State

I found myself winding down easily after my routine. My mind was at ease as bedtime neared, and there were no anxious thoughts or lingering worries to disturb my sleep preparation. Physical tiredness was present but felt wholesome rather than draining.

5. Summary & Reflection

General Well-Being

Today was marked by tangible stability and even modest improvement in both my physical comfort and mental clarity. The day started with only mild stiffness, which quickly faded, and notably, at no point did I experience significant pain, imbalance, or digestive upset. My energy, especially in the afternoon, was higher than usual—a trend I attribute to more restful sleep, better hydration, and careful adherence to my dietary guidelines.

My appetite was steady throughout the day, and I enjoyed each meal free from discomfort or indigestion. The inclusion of regular, structured activities—whether exercise, reading, or family engagement—kept me both mentally active and emotionally uplifted.

Trends and Personal Observations

The increased energy, brighter mood, and improved alertness are encouraging. I am convinced that sticking to consistent routines—particularly regular sleep, balanced meals, and daily exercise—is paying dividends. My voice is also recovering well; increasing my water intake and resting my vocal cords are proving helpful.

Lessons and Thoughts for Tomorrow

Small, steady improvements are possible with sustained attention to daily habits. Social interaction and intellectual stimulation continue to enrich my days, and I am reminded how vital it is to weave these elements intentionally into my routine. Going forward, I intend to reinforce these positive choices and continue pacing my activity sensibly.

Next Steps & Recommendations

I plan to maintain my current approach: balanced diet, hydration, daily movement, and regular sleep hours. I will also prioritize voice rest and try warm saline gargles tomorrow to accelerate recovery. It’s important that I consistently monitor my vitals—blood pressure and blood sugar—so I’ll make a point to record those this week. If the joint stiffness worsens or my vocal hoarseness persists, I will consult my physician or specialist accordingly. No urgent medical concerns have emerged.
