# Justice Arvind Prakash Mehra – Daily Health Log Entry and Template (August 21, 2024)

## Overview

As someone committed to discipline and thorough documentation, I’ve developed this daily health log both as a personal record and as a robust template, reflecting the standards I uphold as a judicial professional. This format allows me to keep a precise account of my health events, medications, and routines, while ensuring that every entry is traceable, clear, and suitable for retrospective analysis. Consistent record-keeping is essential not only for effective health monitoring but also for maintaining accountability, especially in matters that might require review or legal scrutiny.

## Structured Daily Health Log Template

I’ve organized the log into clearly defined sections. Each entry is recorded with an exact timestamp and covers symptoms, medications, activities, missed routines, and any contextual information that might be relevant. My intention is to ensure that every detail is captured accurately, enabling me or any reviewer to easily trace patterns, compliance, and interventions.

### Log Sections

- **Date**: Full date in DD/MM/YYYY format.
- **Time**: 24-hour time for each event or intervention.
- **Symptoms Experienced**: Specific symptoms, time of onset, and a severity rating (Visual Analogue Scale, VAS).
- **Activities with Location**: What I did and where, with relevant times.
- **Medications Taken**: Name, dosage, method, time, and details of any missed doses.
- **Additional Notes**: Other relevant observations, environment, and actions taken.
- **Missed Routine**: Noting any missed medication, entries, or routines for compliance and review.
- **Signature/Initials**: Authentication of the record.

## Reflections on Documentation Practice

I’ve long believed that health logs should meet the same standards of accuracy and clarity as any legal document. Each entry in my log includes detailed descriptors, standard severity scales, and time-stamped authentication, mirroring the principles I apply in legal practice. Recording activities with precise locations and times allows me to analyze possible causal links between my environment and symptoms, and to identify patterns that might inform future care.

If I miss a medication or forget a log entry, I make sure to note it explicitly and provide an explanation or corrective action. This level of openness lets me maintain compliance and accountability—critical for both health and professional integrity. I also note environmental factors, such as extreme heat or humidity, which may contribute to symptoms. Documenting these details helps me, and any medical professional who reviews my records, better understand the broader context and potential triggers.

Every log entry is authenticated with my signature, ensuring the records can be relied upon in both medical and legal reviews.
