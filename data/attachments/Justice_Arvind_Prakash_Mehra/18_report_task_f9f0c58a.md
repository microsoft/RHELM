- **Review of Program Outcomes:** Evaluation of accomplishments, adherence to judicial mentoring principles, and discussion of challenges faced during the year.
- **Closure Logistics:** Outlining procedures for archiving documentation, digital correspondence, and preparing reports required by the Bar Council and National Judicial Academy.

- **Ethical & Procedural Oversight:** Ensuring all steps comply with legal ethics, confidentiality protocols, and procedural requirements governing judicial mentoring.
- **Follow-up Planning:** Strategies for collecting feedback, future improvements, and establishing a process for retrospective review at the annual meeting.

## Communication Accommodations & Ethical Considerations

This session presented unique logistical considerations due to Justice Mehra’s vocal rest. Throughout the meeting, he communicated his observations and guidance via digital written notes (iMessage, email) and supplemented interactions with text-to-speech tools.

- **Transparency:** All participants had direct and equal access to Justice Mehra’s contributions, ensuring a fair and inclusive process.
- **Record Keeping:** Every written exchange was carefully archived according to professional standards. Access controls and encryption were applied to all sensitive materials.
- **Confidentiality:** Measures included password protection and periodic audits, in strict adherence to the Bar Council’s confidentiality and data protection policies.
- **Ethical Compliance:** By making reasonable accommodations for medical needs, the program maintained a high standard of professionalism and inclusivity. This aligns with the spirit and requirements of legal ethics protocols governing professional conduct and non-discrimination.
