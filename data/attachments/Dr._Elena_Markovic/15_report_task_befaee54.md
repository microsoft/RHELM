## 3. Discussion Summary

### 3.1 LunaLink Project Progress

Dr. Markovic presented the latest achievements on the LunaLink satellite bus, which successfully completed phase C (detailed design). All engineering documentation requirements, in accordance with ECSS-E-ST-10C, have been met. The project also progressed through essential thermal vacuum and functional verification tests in Q4 2023, recorded under Work Package LL-SYS-DV-21. All previously open Tier 1 items from the last annual review (see Issue Log LL-2023-REV) have now been addressed.

A technical overview emphasized enhancements in subsystem integration, particularly the deployment of modular avionics. Integration of the electrical and mechanical subsystems was noted as a significant advancement for overall system reliability. However, qualification of specific payload components has encountered minor delays due to vendor delivery timelines, and this item has been escalated in the risk management system for corrective action.

### 3.2 Systems Engineering Milestones

The team has accomplished statically traceable integration between hardware and software, verified under ECSS-M-ST-40C protocols. A major architectural transition saw the primary data bus reconfigured to comply with ECSS-E-ST-50C standards, ensuring robust interoperability across modules. Full requirements traceability has now been established and is managed through the Issue Tracking System (ITS #LUNA-ENG-2024-05), providing a solid foundation for future internal and external audits.

Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) tools were further deployed, which has increased agility across engineering and operations teams. ESA’s internal audit group endorsed the MBSE feature set, noting improvements in cross-functional collaboration and workflow transparency.

### 3.3 Advanced Engineering & Technology Challenges

A key discussion focused on the adoption of next-generation composite thermal shielding at Technology Readiness Level 7 (TRL-7). Further testing is required in collaboration with EU industry partners, with preliminary feasibility planning underway. Bottlenecks at subsystem interfaces, particularly around real-time data synchronization, have surfaced, highlighting the need for improved integration procedures.

Mitigation strategies for deep-space communication modules are under development, with processes referenced against the ECSS-E-ST-30C risk documentation. The team is additionally exploring adaptive protocols to support more efficient data exchange and reliability for future long-duration missions.

### 3.4 Cross-Agency Systems Integration Workshop Proposal

To further strengthen interoperability and knowledge sharing, Dr. Markovic proposed a cross-agency workshop aimed at the LunaLink-HERMES joint mission. The workshop’s primary goals are the harmonization of system requirements between ESA and EU partners, clarifying approval pathways for joint subsystem testing, and creating a forum to exchange insights drawn from previous multi-agency integration projects.

Resource needs were reviewed and are pending clearance from project leadership and key coordination leads. Effectiveness of the workshop will be measured against agreed success metrics, defined in Memorandum of Understanding #ESA-EU-2023-07.

### 3.5 Leadership Development

Dr. Markovic requested to participate in ESA's "Emerging Technical Leaders" program, emphasizing the importance of developing further skills in technical team management and inter-agency negotiation. HR Manager Ms. van Leeuwen is coordinating the application, ensuring all necessary documentation is completed before the nomination window closes.

To assess the impact of leadership development, quarterly performance reviews will be used, focusing on teamwork effectiveness and negotiation outcomes.

### 3.6 Professional Growth Objectives

For 2024, Dr. Markovic established the following personal and team goals:

- Complete certification in systems integration per ECSS E40C standards
- Launch an internal mentorship program for junior engineers to enhance skill development and succession planning
- Lead the publication of LunaLink technical findings in peer-reviewed journals, aiming to bolster ESA’s reputation in satellite systems engineering
- Strengthen collaboration across departments to drive innovation and accelerate project timelines

Active engagement in these objectives is expected to further build technical excellence and foster talent within the agency.
