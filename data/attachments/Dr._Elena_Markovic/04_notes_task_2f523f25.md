## 2. Product Highlights and Reasons Behind My Choices

### Fresh, In-Season Vegetables: My Winter Staples

In January, I lean into the Dutch winter harvest. Kale, parsnips, leek, Brussels sprouts, and carrots are not just affordable—they're fresher and taste best right now. Kale and spinach are both great for quick, nutritious meals. If I can’t find kale, spinach or even Swiss chard does the trick. Parsnip sometimes runs out, so sweet potato or celeriac make good substitutes in a pinch.

Tomatoes, though not strictly in season, are consistently available (usually greenhouse-grown). They add much-needed color and brightness to my meals.

### Prioritizing Protein and Recovery: Greek Yogurt & Eggs

After active days like today, I go for high-protein, low-fat options to recover properly. Albert Heijn's own Greek yogurt (Griekse Yoghurt 0% Vet) has about 9–10g protein per 100g and virtually no fat—so it's perfect with fruit or seeds for a filling breakfast or post-workout snack. If the 0% version is missing, the 2% one is almost identical in protein, just a touch richer. [Details here][1] and [here][2].

Eggs are an everyday essential; they’re endlessly versatile and keep well.

### Convenience Without Compromise

Wholemeal wraps and bread make meal-prep ultra fast. I often throw roasted veggies, salad, or an egg scramble into a wrap for an instant lunch, or slice up apples onto toast with some nut butter for a snack. The Bonduelle mixed pulses let me boost fiber and plant protein in salads or even warm veggie bowls. Pumpkin seeds are a little extra I like to keep on hand: they’re a simple topping for yogurt, cereal, or salads that add crunch and nutrients (especially magnesium).

### Something New for Mind and Body: Pure Leaf “Tulsi Zen” Tea

Aisha’s tip really paid off—I discovered this Tulsi Zen herbal infusion, which has quickly become my wind-down cup after a busy day. Tulsi is known for its calming properties and, since the blend is caffeine-free (with chamomile and citrus hints), it fits perfectly for relaxation after sports or before bed. The pyramid bags let the tea infuse fully, unlocking the flavors. If Albert Heijn happens to be out, I’ll go for another Pure Leaf blend or a Pukka Relax box instead. [See product][3]

### Hydration Ready—All Week

Spa Reine mineral water in half-liter bottles is a convenient grab-and-go solution, especially when I’m out running errands or want to rehydrate straight after an active event. I stock up knowing it’ll all get used between work, training sessions, and relaxing at home.

## 3. My Go-To Meal Ideas and In-Store Strategies

**Speed and Freshness First**:  
When shopping, I reach for pre-packed bags of greens when I know the week ahead will be hectic. They last a little longer and make it easier to whip up a meal last minute.

**A Few Favorite Combos:**  
- **Quick Wrap:** Spread hummus on a wholemeal wrap, pile on spinach, sliced boiled eggs, and veggies—done in minutes.
- **Morning Bowl:** Greek yogurt, diced pears or apples, pumpkin seeds, and a drizzle of honey for a breakfast that keeps me going until lunchtime.
- **Batch Roasted Roots:** I’ll roast extra Brussels sprouts, carrots, and parsnip on Sunday evening, then use them all week—tossed into wraps, on toast, or as a side.

**Hydration Habit:**  
Keeping a couple of those Spa bottles in my bag means I never get caught out without a drink, especially when leaving the gym or heading out early.

## 4. Budget Overview and Substitution Plan

I keep an eye on the total price at checkout. For this list, my regular shop comes to about **€35–€40**, breaking down roughly as follows:

- **Veg & fruit:** €10–12
- **Greek yogurt:** ~€2
- **Eggs:** ~€3
- **Wraps, bread, hummus:** €6–8
- **Herbal tea:** €3–4
- **Mineral water:** €3.50
- **Pulses, seeds, canned items:** ~€5

**If something's missing…**  
- **Greens:** I swap spinach for kale or a pre-washed lettuce if stock is low.
- **Yogurt:** Skyr is always a solid alternative—similar protein-to-calorie balance.
- **Bread/Wraps:** I’ll quickly choose a different wholegrain style from the bakery aisle.
- **Fruit/veg:** If tomatoes look tired, I’ll grab bell pepper or cucumber instead.
- **Tea:** Any Pure Leaf or Pukka "Relax" is a safe back-up if my preferred Tulsi Zen is out.
