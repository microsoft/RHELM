## Home Management and Ongoing Care Checklist

To continue supporting Luna’s health and maintain a high quality of life, the following daily and weekly routines are advised:

- **Food & Hydration:** Maintain strict adherence to the prescription renal diet. Ensure fresh water is always available at multiple spots throughout the home.
- **Daily Intake Monitoring:** Keep track of Luna’s eating and drinking habits, noting any decrease or abnormal behavior.
- **Enrichment:**  
    - Rotate toys every 2–3 days to keep activities novel.
    - Incorporate the interactive feather-and-ball toy into daily playtime.
- **Play and Stimulation:** Aim for two gentle play sessions daily (10–15 minutes each), tailored to Luna’s energy level.
- **Litterbox Monitoring:** Observe urine frequency, volume, and consistency; changes could indicate CKD progression or a need for prompt veterinary review.
- **Supplies Inventory:** Review stocks of food and supplements weekly to avoid lapses in essential supplies.
- **Symptom Surveillance:** Be alert for vomiting, diarrhea, weight loss, decreased appetite, lethargy, changes in breathing, or any unusual behaviors.

    - Fluctuations in kidney function tests (creatinine, BUN, SDMA)
    - Appetite, food and water intake
    - Body weight and condition
    - Interest in play and environmental engagement, especially any reduction which could signal discomfort or illness
- **Daily Log:** Maintain a written or digital health journal for Luna, logging her food intake, activity levels, and play behaviors. Any deviations—such as missed meals, excessive drinking or urination, vomiting, or behavioral changes—should be recorded with specifics and brought to the next veterinary visit.
- **Clinical Signs Warranting Immediate Attention:**  
    - Sudden loss of appetite or marked increase in thirst/urination
    - New or worsening vomiting or diarrhea
    - Onset of lethargy, difficulty moving, or weight changes
    - Signs of oral pain, reluctance to eat, or changes in grooming
    - Sudden unexplained changes in behavior
