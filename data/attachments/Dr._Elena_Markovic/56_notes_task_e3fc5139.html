Preparation Notes

Ensuring a smooth event calls for careful coordination and advance planning. Below are the steps we'll follow for procurement, kitchen safety, and prep logistics:

Ingredient Procurement

  • Orders for imported Serbian and Dutch specialty items will be placed no later than 28 June. This allows enough time for shipping and any potential shortages.
  • Standard groceries—including fresh produce and dairy alternatives—will be purchased locally during 10–12 July.

Make-Ahead and Fresh Items

  • Ajvar and Raspberry Cordial can be made up to two days before (between 11–12 July).
  • Gluten-free bread, beef goulash, and apple pie should be prepared the day before (12 July), giving them time to set and flavors to develop.
  • Desserts (chocolate mousse, plum dumplings) will be made or assembled early on the day of the event for best texture and taste.

Kitchen Organization and Allergen Safety

  • Gluten-free dishes will be prepared on designated surfaces and with separate utensils to prevent cross-contamination.
  • A nut-free zone will be created in the prep area for Luka’s safety, with separate cooking and storage solutions.
  • Every dish served will be clearly marked with allergen and dietary labels to guide all guests.

Timings for Hot Dishes and Salads

  • Beef goulash needs to begin simmering by 11:00 am on the event day to reach full tenderness.
  • Grilled vegetables and mashed potatoes are best made just before serving to maintain their flavor and warmth.
  • Salads will be dressed at the very last minute, keeping everything crisp and fresh for the meal.

Risk Management & Contingency Plans

Ingredient Shortages or Delays

  • We will maintain a backup list of local and online suppliers for key ingredients such as gluten-free flour, lactose-free cheese, and imported specialty items.
  • Specialty items are ordered well in advance to provide a buffer for delays.
  • We have developed predefined substitutions for dairy, gluten, and meat in each recipe, so we can adapt quickly if needed.

Scheduling & Team Absences

  • Each critical prep role has an alternate family member ready to step in, ensuring that a last-minute absence won’t stall the schedule.
  • A visible kitchen whiteboard will keep all team members updated with assignments and shifting needs during the event.
  • We'll use our WhatsApp family group for real-time updates, should there be any timing or staffing changes on the day.

Allergy and Cross-Contamination Prevention

  • Color-coded tools and labeled containers will help keep gluten-free and nut-free prepping completely separate from the rest.
  • Prep and cooking areas are split by allergy needs and clearly marked.
  • A check-off protocol before service ensures no allergens slip through or go unnoticed in any dish.
