4. Expanded Risk Assessment

Clear risk identification and mitigation remain fundamental to all ESA technical activities and youth outreach projects ([1], [3]).

Microcontroller Shipment Delay

  • Risk: Delayed delivery of microcontroller units.
  • Impact: The club’s build session depends on these components; any delay could compress the project timeline and disrupt other activities.
  • Mitigation: ESA has established an expedited shipping process with the supplier, aiming for confirmation by 2024-09-10. Jelena will monitor tracking and escalate to procurement if delays continue.

Modular Payload Bay Design

  • Risk: Possible structural or functional failure due to unfamiliar composite configurations.
  • Impact: Could result in payload loss, compromise launch safety, or necessitate design rework.
  • Mitigation: The team will follow ESA’s recommended protocol for material certification and non-destructive testing before incorporating the new modular bay in final build plans.

Cross-Cultural Documentation and Communication

  • Risk: Misunderstandings or overlooked details stemming from language barriers or uneven technical terminology.
  • Impact: Reduced engagement, missed learning objectives, and increased technical errors.
  • Mitigation: Mrs. van Dijk is leading the creation of bilingual session guides and a shared glossary, supporting inclusive discussion and documentation for all team members.

Avionics Handling & ESD Protection

  • Risk: Inadequate ESD safeguards could cause microcontroller or avionics failures.
  • Impact: Component damage and unnecessary downtime.
  • Mitigation: Jelena will revise SOPs, introduce anti-static handling standards, and organize periodic ESD audits to ensure compliance and safety.

6. Closing Remarks and Outcomes

At the conclusion of the meeting, all participants reaffirmed their commitment to the revised build session date, which will only proceed if both shipping and technical milestones are met. Owners for each action item and timeline have been clearly identified, in alignment with the European Cooperation for Space Standardization (ECSS) guidelines.

The group reached a consensus on several priorities: - Focusing on bilingual and accessible documentation to foster teamwork and inclusivity. - Proceeding with the modular payload bay concept only after rigorous ESA risk review and certification. - Updating ESD protocols and emphasizing hands-on safety training for all club members. - Maintaining a transparent record of technical suggestions and risk discussions in future meetings.

Recommendations for the coming weeks include ongoing documentation of project developments for audit and learning purposes, scheduling online sessions to mitigate the impact of supply or logistics delays, and requesting periodic ESA Academy feedback to strengthen STEM engagement.

Key decisions: - Confirmed microcontroller testing and shipping escalation procedure. - Build session tentatively rescheduled to 2024-09-18, subject to component delivery. - Conditional approval of the modular payload bay design, pending successful certification and risk assessment. - Launch of accessible, bilingual documentation initiative. - ESD handling protocols standardized for all future avionics builds.
