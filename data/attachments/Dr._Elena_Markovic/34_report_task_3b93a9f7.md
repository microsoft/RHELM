# Formal Absence Report  
## Mapping App Feedback Session – ESA Technical Aerospace Project

**Document Reference:** ESA-MBMAP-FRS-2024-04-13  
**Submission Date:** 2024-04-13

---

## 1. Session Overview

The Mapping App Feedback Session was scheduled for April 13, 2024, from 09:00 to 10:30 UTC, hosted via ESA's Secure Virtual Collaboration platform (Webex). Dr. Lukas van Rijn, Senior Software Integration Manager, served as facilitator. Elena Markovic, Lead Spacecraft Systems Engineer, was expected to provide essential feedback on the validation of new mapping features. Elena’s input is integral to project advancement, particularly for ensuring the mapping app aligns with spacecraft systems requirements.

---

## 2. Reason for Absence

Elena Markovic was unavailable for the scheduled session due to an unforeseen personal emergency. Early that morning, her dog Luna experienced a sudden and severe illness, requiring immediate veterinary care. Elena promptly reported her absence through the ESA Internal Risk/Event Portal in accordance with [ECSS-Q-ST-10-09C section 4.2](https://ecss.nl/standard/ecss-q-st-10-09c-personnel-and-contingency-management/), meeting the requirements for timely notification of personnel events affecting project deliverables. Given Elena’s role as Lead Spacecraft Systems Engineer—a Tier-1 position with direct responsibility for mapping app validation—a full impact and mitigation review was conducted as required by ESA contingency management protocols. Steps for transparent documentation and prompt resource allocation were immediately initiated.

---

## 3. Impact on Project

### 3.1 Developer Deliverables  
The absence of Markovic’s critical feedback resulted in a delay in the scheduled submission of validation data to the mapping app development team. As a direct consequence, the planned code review and subsequent integration of new mapping features—key to enhancing the ground systems interface—could not proceed according to established timelines.

### 3.2 Project Workflow  
The feedback loop, an essential element in iterative software development, was disrupted. Dependencies involving mapping logic, validation of front-end user experience, and real-time data integration remain on hold until Markovic’s review is received. The absence of completed validation also pauses follow-up actions related to these dependencies, impacting coordination across teams.

### 3.3 Data Pipeline  
As feedback was not provided, data ingestion and validation for real-world mapping scenarios remain incomplete. The issue tracker registered no new mapping issues for this session, which means that potential bugs or design faults could go unnoticed, posing a latent risk to future integration phases.

### 3.4 Risk Mitigation  
Consistent with [ECSS-M-ST-80 risk management standards](https://ecss.nl/standard/ecss-m-st-80-risk-management/), Elena's absence has been formally documented. Interim responsibility for feedback review was assigned to the Deputy Systems Engineer, allowing for preliminary input. However, the depth and technical specificity typically contributed by Markovic were not fully replicated, making rescheduling and full feedback collection a priority.

### 3.5 Project Timeline Implications  
Given the lack of feedback, integration and validation milestones for Q2 2024 will require updating. Current projections estimate a two-working-day delay in related deliverables. There is potential for these delays to cascade into subsequent phases, such as user story testing and certification review, unless mitigation measures—like expedited feedback resubmission—are enacted promptly.

---

## 4. Next Steps and Recommendations

To address both immediate and longer-term impacts of the absence, the following actions are scheduled:

- **Rescheduling:**  
  A follow-up Mapping App Feedback Session is tentatively set for April 16, 2024. The latest build (v1.2) and all updated documentation will be distributed to Elena in advance to facilitate her review.

- **Catch-up Methodology:**  
  Elena will provide her feedback asynchronously through the ESA Secure Portal by April 15, 2024. The Deputy Systems Engineer will co-review and integrate these findings, ensuring continuity in the mapping app tracker and minimizing workflow disruption.

- **Preventive Strategies:**  
  - Implement mandatory pre-confirmation of attendance for all Tier-1 feedback sessions one week prior to meeting dates.
  - Circulate all key session materials at least 48 hours before each session, ensuring participants have sufficient preparation time.
  - Establish systematic cross-backup protocols for all Tier-1 roles, as outlined in [ECSS-Q-ST-10-09C contingency management](https://ecss.nl/standard/ecss-q-st-10-09c-personnel-and-contingency-management/).
  - Enhance advance communication and tracking through the ESA collaborative calendar (SharePoint), supporting greater transparency and preparedness.

These measures are designed to maintain project momentum, reinforce risk management, and prevent recurrence of similar disruptions.
