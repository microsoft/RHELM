Medical Rationale:
The primary aim behind reducing Luna’s medications is to avoid unnecessary overlap and reduce the risk of negative side effects associated with taking multiple drugs. Adjusting her meal frequency also helps her better tolerate food post-medication and can smooth out fluctuations in appetite. While her hydration is sound at present, it remains important to track intake and output, as post-medication appetite shifts may affect her fluid balance.

Urgency and Safety Planning:
Although no immediate clinical concerns were raised, Dr. van der Meer instructed that I should contact the clinic without delay if Luna’s appetite or water intake falls by more than 30% in a day, or if new vomiting or marked lethargy appears. These points are marked as urgent triggers to watch for until the next review.
