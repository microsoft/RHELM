## Occupational Therapy and Ergonomics: Progress and Adjustments

**14:00 – Occupational Therapy: Wrist Rehab**  
I met with Ms. van der Laan to review the latest phase of my wrist rehabilitation. The focus was on three sets of flexion and extension using a resistance band, twelve reps per set, with a minute’s rest. I didn’t experience pain, and my range of motion improved by 8° since last week. My grip strength also went up by 6%.

**14:25 – Ergonomic Adjustments**  
We ran a quick audit of my workstation. Raising the desk height helped me keep a neutral wrist angle, and tilting the monitor slightly forward (by about 4°) made an immediate difference—no more craning my neck forward. EMG readings on my wrist extensors fell by 18% while typing, a clear signal that the adjustments paid off.

**14:40 – Evaluating New Keyboard Hardware**  
I started testing a split mechanical keyboard, with a tented angle of 9°. My shoulders felt noticeably less tense, and the key positioning helped reduce strain on my wrists. It’s taking a little time to adjust—my typing speed is around 90% of normal so far—but I already sense this will help reduce fatigue. My plan is to track adaptation and comfort for the next five workdays.
