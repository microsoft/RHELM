## Reflections on Nutrition and Routine

### Breakfast

The absence of breakfast has become a recurring theme on mornings when deadlines or early meetings take center stage. Over time, I’ve noticed how it sets the tone—by late morning, I tend to feel distracted, reaching for extra coffee to compensate. I’m well aware that eating breakfast improves cognitive sharpness and mood, so recommitting to a consistent morning meal remains a work in progress for me.

### Lunch

I’ve found that a plant-centered lunch, like today’s, translates to sustained energy and better concentration through the afternoon. Lentils bring both protein and fiber, while the grilled vegetables offer antioxidants and a feeling of lightness. Eating in the canteen is a ritual: even when I’m pressed for time, sharing a table with coworkers anchors the day and offers a bit of much-needed levity.

### Dinner

Our dinner tonight wasn’t just a meal—it was a celebration. The mezze was nourishing, but more than that, the meal signified shared success. As conversations moved from shop talk to personal stories, I felt a strong sense of belonging and relaxation. Social meals like this rejuvenate me, offering far more than nutritional value by strengthening the relationship network that supports me in my professional life.
