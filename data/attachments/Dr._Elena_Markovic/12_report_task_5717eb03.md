## 2. Executive Summary

This notice serves to formally announce the postponement of the ESA–JAXA Risk Assessment Report Review, originally scheduled for 26 January 2024. The delay has become necessary due to an unforeseen personal emergency—urgent veterinary care required for Luna, a support animal vital to our project staff’s well-being and effective performance. ESA takes the physical and emotional health of its team members seriously, understanding that prioritizing people is fundamental to sustaining both quality and momentum in critical collaborative projects.

Our commitment to the shared objectives with JAXA remains firm, and all engineering standards and procedures continue to be rigorously observed. Interim measures have been implemented to uphold project progress and ensure that the review rescheduling will not compromise integrity, transparency, or technical completeness. By adjusting our timeline, we aim to allow for thorough risk management evaluation and the seamless assimilation of technical recommendations—adhering closely to ESA’s internal protocols and ECSS standards throughout the transition.

## 4. Next Steps and Contingency Planning

Maintaining project continuity and ensuring a robust review process are top priorities. The following actions outline our approach during the postponement period:

### 4.1 Rescheduling and Logistics Coordination

- The new review date is set for 5 February 2024.
- All designated team members are asked to acknowledge the revised schedule within two working days.
- ESA’s internal protocols will be used to update calendar invitations and associated meeting resources, making certain all participants receive accurate and timely information.

### 4.2 Ongoing Technical and Risk Management Activities

- Interim risk analyses will proceed as planned, with bi-weekly status updates circulated to all stakeholders.
- Documentation of all technical work will conform to the procedures outlined in ESA/ENG/PROC/2022.
- Any modifications to review scope or technical content will be traced and clearly referenced in project records.

### 4.3 Documentation Timeline

- The new submission deadline for supporting documents is 31 January 2024.
- An updated review packet will be provided to team members and stakeholders by 2 February 2024.
- All documentation prepared during this period will be evaluated in accordance with the ESA Risk Governance Guidelines.

### 4.4 Compliance and Governance Protocols

- All actions related to review management and change control will adhere strictly to ECSS-M-ST-80C standards.
- In all interim communications, references to the ESA Risk Governance Guidelines will be included to reinforce shared understanding and accountability.
- Full transparency will be maintained, with all developments openly documented and circulated as appropriate.

**Actionable Recommendations:**

- Responsible parties should verify the status of any pending items before the new review date.
- Project managers will continue actively monitoring for new risks, particularly those emerging from schedule modifications.
- Updates to documentation must clearly mark revisions, following version control procedures stated in ESA policy.
