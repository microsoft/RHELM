## Recipe Highlight: Mediterranean Vegetable Stew

*Serves 2*

**Ingredients:**  
- 1 tbsp olive oil  
- 50g onion (chopped)  
- 1 garlic clove (minced)  
- 1 cup eggplant (cubed)  
- 1 cup zucchini (cubed)  
- ½ cup bell pepper (chopped)  
- 1 cup canned chopped tomatoes  
- ¾ cup canned chickpeas, rinsed  
- 2 tsp fresh or dried herbs (oregano, thyme, parsley)  
- Salt and black pepper to taste  
- 100g low-fat Greek yogurt (per serving)  
- Fresh parsley, chopped, for garnish  

**Preparation:**  
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add the onion and garlic, sauté until softened and fragrant.  
2. Stir in the eggplant, zucchini, and bell pepper. Sauté for about 5 minutes until they begin to soften.  
3. Add the tomatoes and chickpeas. Simmer uncovered for about 20 minutes, stirring occasionally, until vegetables are tender and the flavors meld.  
4. Season with herbs, salt, and pepper.  
5. Spoon into bowls and top each with Greek yogurt and a sprinkle of parsley.

Cooking this stew was the perfect wind-down for a full day—it’s easy enough for a weeknight but still feels special, particularly with thick Greek yogurt for creaminess and protein.
