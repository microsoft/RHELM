Meal Prep Notes

Meals Prepared (May 4, 2024)

  • Grilled Chicken and Spinach-Quinoa Bowls
  • Sweet Potato Hash with Poached Eggs and Fresh Basil
  • Greek Yogurt Parfait with Mixed Berries and Almond Butter
  • Steamed Broccoli and Brown Rice

After a couple of busy weeks, I’m getting back into my weekend meal prep. Having meals ready for the week always makes my mornings less frantic and helps me stick to healthier choices.

Ingredient Selection and Sourcing – My Approach

  • Leafy Greens & Vegetables:
    Organic baby spinach, broccoli, and sweet potatoes anchor most of my dishes. These are loaded with micronutrients, fiber, and antioxidants, and by choosing trustworthy, certified organic suppliers, I avoid most contaminants and feel good about what goes into my body.
  • Chicken Breast:
    I gravitate toward free-range, antibiotic-free chicken. Not only does it taste better, but supporting higher welfare standards also matters to me. Plus, it gives me lean, reliable protein for the week.
  • Whole Grains:
    For complex carbs, I rotate between quinoa and brown rice; both are naturally gluten-free and have a moderate impact on blood sugar. Sourcing from bulk batches with traceability ensures that flavors and cooking times remain consistent.
  • Cage-Free Eggs:
    Not just for breakfast—these eggs are a nutrient powerhouse (think choline and vitamin D). Buying from local, transparent farms means I know they’re fresh and responsibly sourced.
  • Greek Yogurt & Almond Butter:
    These add the finishing touch to breakfasts and snacks. Their high protein and healthy fats keep me full, and I appreciate that my suppliers prioritize cold storage to preserve freshness.
  • Herbs & Berries:
    Fresh basil and a rotating selection of in-season berries bring variety and color to each meal, and by shopping local I keep my kitchen as pesticide-free as possible.
  • Olive Oil:
    I’m particular about extra-virgin olive oil. I like the peppery finish, and I feel reassured by brands with documented purity. It’s my go-to for everything from sautéing to dressing salads.
  • Luna’s Prescription Foods:
    Luna’s health is non-negotiable. She’s on a strict prescription diet due to underlying health concerns, so I only purchase from trusted veterinary pharmacies. I always track lot numbers for accountability, especially since pet food recalls have become more publicized.

A Few Personal Observations

This week’s meal prep went pretty smoothly, especially once I got into a rhythm. I tackled all the chopping and initial prep in one go, which saved a lot of back-and-forth—and, honestly, significantly reduced clean-up time.

Luna, as always, was my shadow in the kitchen. She’s especially interested when there’s chicken around, so I set up a removable barrier to keep her away from the cooking zone. It’s a simple step, but it keeps things safe for both of us.

Labeling all containers and keeping an updated ingredient list nearby helps me stay organized and prevents any confusion, especially since Luna has specific dietary needs. There was a slight hiccup when my basil delivery was delayed, but adapting on the fly (staging meals and prepping other elements first) meant I didn’t lose much time.

Analytical Review: Meal Prep Efficiency and Process Improvements

Taking a more methodical approach has paid off this week:

  • Batch Prep:
    By grouping all washing, chopping, and storage tasks at the start, I shaved about 20% off my usual active time. Prepping grains and proteins in parallel proved especially efficient.

  • Quality Assurance:
    Staying loyal to suppliers with transparent batch controls has virtually eliminated spoilage issues. All this week’s ingredients were in excellent condition.

  • Inventory and Tracking:
    Keeping a standardized ingredient log and noting lot numbers—especially for Luna’s prescription food—makes it easy to verify sources quickly or respond in case of any issues.

  • Pet Considerations:
    Maintaining a strict pet-free zone during meal prep has reduced distractions and the risk of cross-contamination. Luna’s curiosity about poultry is ongoing, so I’ll likely add a scheduled treat regimen next week to keep her occupied.

  • Kitchen Safety Upgrades:
    Adding extra sets of utensils and dedicated cutting boards for produce and proteins made the workflow smoother and safer.

  • Product Evaluations:
    I tried a new batch of almond butter this week—no weird oil separation, and all supplier certifications checked out. I’ll keep monitoring for consistency, but so far, it’s earned a repeat spot on my list.

Overall, these small adjustments have made a noticeable difference, both in efficiency and peace of mind.
