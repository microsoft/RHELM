## Dietary & Nutritional Considerations

Supporting the sustained focus and vitality required in our work calls for well-balanced meals. Today’s menu thoughtfully combines lean proteins, complex carbohydrates, healthy fats, and abundant vegetables and fruit, reflecting both Mediterranean culinary traditions and modern nutritional science.

- **Protein** sources like salmon, Greek yogurt, and chickpeas contribute to muscle repair, mental focus, and lasting fullness—important for days spent on mission planning or technical reviews.
- **Complex carbohydrates and fiber** from quinoa and wholegrain pita help stabilize blood sugar, giving a reliable foundation for attention and physical energy.
- **Healthy fats,** most notably from olive oil and salmon, support brain function and help manage inflammation, which is crucial during prolonged project sprints.
- **Antioxidants** from the colourful vegetables and seasonal fruit bolster cellular health and immune function.
- **Calcium and B vitamins** in feta and yogurt nourish the nervous system, promoting resilience under stress.
- **Hydration** is boosted naturally through a menu rich in fresh produce and salads.

**Allergen & Substitution Summary:**  
This menu avoids common allergens like nuts but does contain gluten (in pita) and dairy (in yogurt and feta); plant-based or gluten-free substitutions are simple and listed alongside each dish. The flexibility of Mediterranean cuisine allows group meals to remain inclusive and welcoming to all team members.

References include [Harvard Health Publishing](https://www.health.harvard.edu/newsletter_article/the-mediterranean-diet), [WHO recommendations](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/healthy-diet), [Oldways Mediterranean Diet](https://oldwayspt.org/traditional-diets/mediterranean-diet/), and recent scientific reviews on workplace nutrition.

## Reflection: Culinary Inspiration, Well-being, and Team Building at ESA

Preparing and sharing Mediterranean food in the ESA community has always reminded me why I value our diverse, international teams. The act of planning a meal together—deciding on dishes, shopping, prepping, and then sharing a table—echoes much of what we do as engineers and collaborators. It isn’t only about nourishing the body; it’s a social ritual that strengthens trust, celebrates cultural heritage, and lightens the highs and lows of demanding technical work.

The interplay of vibrant vegetables, aromatic herbs, and healthy oils in tonight’s meal wasn’t just about flavor. I noticed the genuine enjoyment team members took in trying each other's favorite combinations, swapping ingredient tips, and adapting recipes to include everyone at the table. These moments of culinary exchange foster openness, deepen respect, and encourage us to see problem solving—and each other—in new light. In practice, the habits we develop around food—bringing in flexibility for vegan or gluten-free needs, or taking the extra time to grill a vegetable to perfection—translate directly to the care and adaptability we need as a cross-cultural, problem-solving team.

After a meal like this, I feel recharged, but it’s more than just physical energy. There’s a subtle mental reset that comes from laughter around the table and that rare, collective pause in our busy schedules. Conversation seems to flow more freely—project issues, life updates, even a funny moment about Luna’s latest cat-antics. I’m convinced these meals fuel more than just our bodies; they’re essential to the empathy, creativity, and shared purpose that drive ESA’s ongoing success.
