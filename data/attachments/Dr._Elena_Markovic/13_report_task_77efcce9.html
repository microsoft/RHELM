Model Rocketry Workshop – Safety Checklist and Inventory Report

Event: Model Rocketry Workshop
Date: January 27th, 2024
Location: Noordwijk Community Center
Organizer: Dr. Elena Markovic, Lead Spacecraft Systems Engineer, European Space Agency (ESA)

Executive Summary

The Model Rocketry Workshop gathered aerospace enthusiasts, students, and community members to participate in hands-on activities centered around the design, assembly, and launch of model rockets. Safety, regulatory compliance, and participant support were prioritized throughout the event, reflecting both European Space Agency (ESA) best practices and wider European aerospace industry standards.

Our workshop aimed to:

  • Implement robust safety protocols to protect both participants and staff during rocket assembly and launches.
  • Comply fully with ESA recommendations concerning educational outreach and technical workshop safety procedures [1].
  • Integrate risk management strategies through staff oversight, thorough documentation, and dedicated processes for any incidents or emergencies.
  • Ensure the program is accessible and inclusive, conforming to ESA policies for fair participation and physical/cognitive accommodations [2].

The safety framework for the event covered all stages and zones, from equipment handling and chemical management to fire prevention and emergency protocols. Clear lines of responsibility, detailed planning, and practical adaptations ensured an engaging experience for all attendees, regardless of background or ability.

Participant Safety and Accessibility Adaptations

Ensuring an inclusive and safe environment was a top priority. The following measures, aligned with ESA and European accessibility directives, were in place to support all participants:

Physical Accessibility:
- Wheelchair ramps were installed at all key access points in the venue. At least one workstation was modified with adjustable heights and wider aisles for ease of movement and assembly by those using mobility aids. - All equipment was kept within comfortable reach zones. Pathways were regularly checked and kept free of obstructions, and tactile warning strips installed in higher-risk areas.

Cognitive Inclusivity:
- Instructional materials were produced in easy-to-read formats, including large print options and visual guides to make technical steps clearer. - Staff completed training on step-by-step guidance delivery and adaptive communication, providing tailored assistance as needed. - Safety briefings and venue signage utilized both written and pictorial cues to ensure comprehension for all literacy levels.

Sensory Considerations:
- Launch times adhered to a noise-reduction schedule, and ear protection was made available for those sensitive to loud sounds. - A scent-free policy for chemicals and cleaning agents was observed, benefiting individuals with allergies or environmental sensitivities.

Emergency Procedures:
- Emergency exit signs and evacuation instructions were posted in large print and color-coded formats, closely matching ESA accessibility recommendations.

Additional accommodations for participants were handled promptly following requests or declared needs, ensuring continued safety and engagement throughout the workshop.
