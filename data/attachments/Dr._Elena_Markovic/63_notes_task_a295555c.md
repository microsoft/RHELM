## Exercise Modifications & Ergonomic Strategies

### Adapting Movement and Workspace for Wrist Health

Living with ongoing wrist strain means I’m constantly troubleshooting my routine. Today, I wore a supportive wrist brace during cycling, computer tasks, piano, and any activity with repetitive motion. This simple measure helps protect the joint and keeps discomfort in check, following advice from JOSPT and my occupational therapist. During yoga, I leaned on blocks and swapped out plank poses for tabletop alternatives—this adjustment makes a significant difference and stays within ACSM’s safe guidelines.  

My workstation at home is set up with a height-adjustable chair, gel wrist rests, and a monitor that sits at eye-level. I’ve found that splitting long work blocks into shorter intervals, and switching up typing technique or device, does wonders for minimizing pain. ESA’s ergonomics protocols, along with Ms. van der Laan’s recommendations, have guided these choices.

### Practical Ergonomics in Everyday Life

I’m vigilant about posture throughout the day—whether at my desk, in the kitchen, or at the piano. Frequent posture checks and a commitment to a neutral wrist position (as emphasized in the HSE guide) have substantially reduced flare-ups. In the kitchen, I’ve swapped standard utensils for wider, padded grips and started prepping meals seated. These small shifts aren’t just about pain prevention, but about making daily life smoother.

## Notes on Piano Practice Technique

Today’s piano session began with slow C major scales, left hand braced, right hand focusing on finger independence. I worked through “Clair de Lune” at a slower tempo, keeping my wrist elevated. Limiting each block to about 20 minutes—and taking five-minute breaks in between—helps my endurance and keeps discomfort at bay. These modifications allow me to enjoy music without compromising my wrist’s recovery.

## Reflections & Evidence-Based Wellness Assessment

### Herbal Tea: Valerian & Lemon Balm

I’ve come to appreciate the gentle support that a cup of valerian and lemon balm tea offers. This morning and midday, the herbal blend provided noticeable relaxation and eased muscle tension—especially after periods of computer work. The transition from intensive technical focus to restorative yoga or family time feels smoother, and I’m convinced by both my own experience and the supporting research that regular use lowers my stress levels and improves sleep quality ([Kennedy et al., 2006; NIH Valerian Fact Sheet]).

### Professional Stress Management

Balancing a demanding engineering role and personal wellness is always a challenge. My interventions—mindful breathing, scheduled movement, keeping my workspace comfortable—are not just habits, but research-supported strategies for stress reduction and focus. Structured breaks and postural awareness, as noted by Sorenson et al. (2020), have genuinely enhanced my concentration and made technical sessions more bearable.

I found mentoring today especially rewarding; the sense of purpose and connection with junior engineers reinforces why social interaction is vital for emotional resilience. Ergonomic adjustments continue to be indispensable—not only for pain management, but for maintaining performance and clear thinking throughout unpredictable workdays.
