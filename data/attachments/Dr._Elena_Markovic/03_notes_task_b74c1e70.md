** *Accessibility:** Evaluated specifically for participants with moderate wrist mobility limitations; considers trail surface, paths, available rest stops, and the need for hand or technical support.

**Summary Recommendation:**  
Both the Noordwijk Forest and Dune Perimeter routes are highly suitable options, scoring well across all categories. They offer sufficient challenge, strong accessibility, and compliance with both ESA and local environmental standards. Final route selection will depend on permit approval and results of the upcoming accessibility audit. Adaptive waypoints will be incorporated in both routes to ensure inclusivity for all team members.

## 3. Logistics Plan and Checklist

### 3.1. Equipment and Gear (All-Weather Preparation)
- Orienteering compasses (offering both traditional and digital/app-based formats, plus tactile options for greater accessibility)
- Large-print, high-contrast maps for ease of reading
- High-visibility safety vests for all participants
- Guidance on weather-appropriate gear (including thermal hats, gloves, waterproof layers)
- Walkie-talkies and/or established group messaging apps for real-time communication
- Branded 1L water bottles for each participant
- Field-portable first aid kits (one kit per team)
- Sun protection and insect repellent
- Wrist or hand support devices (available for any participant who requires them)
- Lightweight folding chairs or stools for scheduled rest stops

### 3.2. Permissions, Approvals, and Regulatory Compliance
- Formal submission to the ESTEC Events Committee for event approval (including completion of required forms and annexes for safety)
- Municipal permit application for routes extending into dune or forest areas (with attention to protected wildlife seasons)
- Review and confirmation of insurance, ensuring coverage for group outdoor activities
- Coordination with the ESA Environmental Officer to ensure Natura 2000 and Dutch flora/fauna regulations are upheld
- GDPR compliance for participant data collected during event registration and communications

### 3.3. Safety, Risk Management, and Medical Support
- Comprehensive route risk assessment (completed and filed with ESTEC HSE at least two weeks prior to the event)
- Emergency access planning, including mapping of entry/exit points and communication with local ambulance services
- Medical support roster, with Dr. Noor van Dijk as the lead and a backup paramedic on-call
- Compliance with COVID-19 and influenza protocols according to the most recent ESA and national guidelines
- Weather monitoring and a 24-hour “go/no-go” decision process based on forecast and safety considerations

### 3.4. Participant Communication
- Distribution of a detailed event information package outlining logistics, safety, route maps, and principles of inclusivity
- Pre-event online survey to gather information on medical needs, dietary requirements, and emergency contacts
- Regular status updates via email, including reminders, confirmations, and any changes
- Accessibility-focused communications, ensuring materials are clear, concise, and available in multiple languages where necessary

### 3.5. Emergency Protocols and Procedures
- Comprehensive emergency contact list, both digital and printed
- On-route check-in via QR codes or SMS at predefined waypoints
- Maps clearly marking evacuation routes and assembly points
- Identification and designation of weather shelters and incident management sites
- Incident reporting process following ESA’s EH&S protocols (Form 7-22 submitted within 24 hours)

## 5. Additional Considerations and Technical Notes

- **Alignment with ESA Wellness Initiatives:**  
  This event directly supports ESA’s ongoing health and well-being programs, providing colleagues with an opportunity for structured physical activity and outdoor engagement. Outcomes and feedback will be shared in the quarterly wellness review.

- **Inclusion and Diversity:**  
  Route selection and event materials are designed to promote accessibility for all, regardless of physical ability. All key information will be bilingual (English/Dutch), and the participant survey includes questions about dietary, religious, and medical needs to accommodate everyone.

- **Leveraging ESA Resources:**  
  All event registrations will use ESA’s internal booking system. The ESA staff mobile app will be utilized for distributing wayfinding information and for timely emergency alerts during the event.

- **Environmental Stewardship:**  
  Strict adherence to Natura 2000 and Dutch environmental requirements is mandatory. “Leave no trace” principles will be communicated to all participants, and waste disposal bags will be provided at each checkpoint.

- **Organizational Learning:**  
  The post-event report, capturing both participant feedback and process adjustments, will be stored on the ESA intranet. These insights will help refine future technical team-building initiatives.

- **Contingency Planning:**  
  If weather or permit issues arise, the event will move to the on-campus route, ensuring that physical activity and team-building objectives are met indoors while maintaining full safety and participation.
