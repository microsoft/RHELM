## 3) What I’ll Improve Next Time

### What to Track (so the record is more useful)  
To make future entries clearer and easier to compare, I’ll capture a few basics every time—even on short sessions:

- **Start/end time or total minutes** (so “shortened” has a number attached)  
- **Pre- and post-session symptom ratings (0–10):**  
  - Sinus pressure/congestion  
  - Headache intensity  
  - Fatigue/energy level  
- **Environment note:** A quick line on humidity/damp air and whether it correlates with worse sinus pressure.

### Adjustments for Congestion or Headache Days  
When I’m foggy or uncomfortable, decision-making is part of the problem. A simple plan will make it easier to practice without negotiating with myself the whole time:

- Keep a **predefined “congested-day gentle sequence”** so I can start immediately without overthinking.  
- Set the expectation upfront: **very low intensity**, comfort-first, nothing goal-driven.  
- Use an intentional stopping rule: **end early when symptoms climb** or after a minimum gentle block, whichever comes first. That prevents frustration and avoids turning yoga into another chore.

### Recovery & Same-Day Support  
Given that this session increased fatigue rather than easing it, I’ll treat symptom-limited practices as a recovery signal:

- Emphasize **hydration and rest** afterward.  
- If a short, gentle practice still drains me, **scale the next session down further** (shorter and simpler).  
- End each entry with a single summary line that captures the net effect, for example: **“Limited relief; fatigue increased; bed early.”**
