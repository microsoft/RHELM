# Desert Conservation Collaboration: Field Protocol Update  
**Date:** 2024-03-06  
**Prepared for:** Advanced Ecological and Management Review  
**Prepared by:** Desert Conservation Collaboration Team

---

## Executive Summary

This protocol update provides significant enhancements to the collaborative field procedures for habitat conservation and stewardship within the Mojave Desert and adjacent sand dune systems. These revisions directly respond to detailed input from Cahuilla Elders and research partners, underscoring our commitment to an evolving, respectful, and rigorously documented approach that integrates Traditional Ecological Knowledge (TEK) with current ecological science.

The central changes focus on:
- Systematic inclusion of Cahuilla Elder recommendations, particularly regarding the identification and stewardship of newly prioritized desert plant species.
- Realignment of stewardship strategies to unify cultural priorities and adaptive ecological management in both planning and execution.
- Adoption of collaborative, multidisciplinary survey methodologies designed to be both ecologically rigorous and culturally sensitive.
- Enhancement of documentation and traceability standards, supporting robust audit processes and transparent communication among all partners.

These updates draw on cross-disciplinary reviews, including team discussions, Elder correspondence, and careful consideration of key scientific publications. All changes reflect our dual commitment to scientific integrity and cultural respect, aiming for seamless integration into ongoing field operations and collaborative reporting.
