## Morning: Pre-sunrise → 7:15 AM (Rainfall Review and Team Update)
I was up before sunrise and working at my desk with coffee, which has become my most reliable way to get uninterrupted thinking time. I pulled up the weekly rainfall data from the Ridgecrest Desert Monitoring Station and settled into a careful review—slow enough to catch small changes, fast enough to keep momentum.

This week’s numbers showed a **slight uptick in precipitation**, not dramatic, but noticeable enough to document cleanly. I built **annotated graphs** so the team could see the trend at a glance and also understand the context behind it—what changed, where it showed up, and why it mattered.

By **7:15 AM**, I sent a group email to **Dr. Priya Rao**, **Emily Tran**, and **Maria Lopez** with the analysis attached. I also called out **one data anomaly** directly in the message. I didn’t want it to get buried in the rest of the update; if something looks off, it’s better to name it early so we can confirm whether it’s a real signal, a sensor issue, or a logging hiccup.

Mid-morning, Oscar came by to take another shot at the cloud-sync issue on my **Google Pixel 7 Pro**. The first round of repairs had helped, but it still hadn’t fully recovered everything I needed. This time, Oscar dug into the sync logs and kept at it until the phone and cloud services finally lined up the way they should.
