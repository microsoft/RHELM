## 9:30 AM Birdwatching Pause (Secondary Observations)
Around **9:30 AM**, I took a dedicated break from tracking to watch and listen for birds. This stop is clearly marked in my notes and supported by a set of landscape photos aimed at capturing habitat context rather than only trying for tight portraits.

### Birds observed
- A **pair of Le Conte’s thrashers**
- A **flock of sage sparrows**

### Documentation
- Written field notes taken during the pause  
- Landscape photographs for habitat and context

### Species notes (general reference only)
- **Le Conte’s thrasher** (*Toxostoma lecontei*): Commonly associated with **desert scrub** and often described as foraging on or near the ground, moving with a relatively **skulking** style through low cover. (General reference: [1])  
- **Sage sparrows (Sagebrush Sparrow complex)** (*Artemisiospiza* spp.; recorded as observed): Commonly associated with **sagebrush/arid shrub** habitats and often seen outside breeding season in **groups/flocks**, foraging on or near the ground. (General reference: [5])

**Taxonomic note:** I wrote these down as “sage sparrows” in the field. Since “Sage Sparrow” has been split in many checklists (often into Sagebrush Sparrow and Bell’s Sparrow), I’m keeping my original label rather than retrofitting an ID I didn’t document at the time. (Reference background: [5])
