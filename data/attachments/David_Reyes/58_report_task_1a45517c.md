## Meeting Summary and Key Outcomes

The field team’s August meeting focused on making Ridgecrest’s outreach strategy more effective and inclusive. Metrics show steady gains in both digital and in-person engagement, especially with younger audiences, but the team acknowledged continued challenges in attracting older adults and those from non-English speaking backgrounds. To address these gaps, members called for more accessible outreach materials and partnerships with local organizations serving these groups.

Review of symposium flier drafts identified several areas for improvement. The team agreed to prioritize visual clarity and readability—for example, by increasing title font sizes and repositioning important event information where it’s easier to spot. Suggestions also included adding visuals of local native flora, expanding the use of infographics and icons, and emphasizing practical information about workshops and event logistics. Incorporating a concise Q&A section into outreach materials emerged as an effective strategy for demystifying ecological concepts and easing public engagement.

Throughout the meeting, the importance of clear demographic tracking was highlighted. Accurate reporting not only informs ongoing outreach strategies but is also crucial for supporting grant applications and future funding cycles.

### Key Suggestions

- Integrate a Q&A infographic with the symposium flier and digital outreach (Priya)
- Incorporate more universal symbols and regional species images to support comprehension for all audiences (Emily, Miguel)
- Prioritize placement and clarity of RSVP and contact information (Maria)
- Test new materials for readability with both younger and older participants, ensuring accessibility across age groups and backgrounds (David)

### Recommendations

- Continue monitoring and analyzing engagement data, focusing on underrepresented ages and language groups
- Standardize the inclusion of one or more key impact statistics in all outward-facing communications
- Expand the use of infographics and visual aids across all public-facing documents to increase accessibility

### Next Steps

- Complete all flier and infographic revisions by August 19  
- Launch the updated digital campaign by August 18  
- Prepare an outreach summary impact report for review at the September steering committee meeting

These action steps support the Ridgecrest Research Center’s commitment to fostering deeper community involvement, broadening public understanding of restoration ecology, and ensuring transparency in all outreach work.
