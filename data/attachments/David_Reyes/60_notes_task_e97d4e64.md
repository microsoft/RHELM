## Breakfast Log — Spicy Black Bean Wrap (Tweak Recorded)
At breakfast I wrote down a small adjustment that made a real difference: **a squeeze of lime and a pinch of smoked paprika stirred into the beans right before assembly**. The lime brightened the filling without changing the overall flavor identity, and the smoked paprika deepened the heat in a way that felt more rounded than simply adding more chili.

| Ingredients | Quantities | Preparation Steps | Notes |
|---|---:|---|---|
