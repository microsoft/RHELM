## Lunch

### Homemade Grilled Tilapia with Snap Peas and Greens

I took extra care planning lunch, knowing I wanted something that supported my sinus health and provided steady energy. Grilled tilapia was the centerpiece, surrounded by crisp snap peas, baby spinach, and a few sustainability-focused tweaks I’ve picked up through my work.

#### Ingredient Sourcing & Sustainability

Most of my produce came from FreshDirect’s recent online delivery. I always check for certifications—USDA Organic and GLOBALG.A.P. for produce, Best Aquaculture Practices (BAP) for fish. The snap peas and greens were grown in California, while the tilapia fillets came from BAP-certified farms in Indonesia (Regal Springs). I pay attention to these details because they mirror my own conservation ethics, both professionally and personally.

#### Preparation & Sustainability Steps

As I prepped lunch, I tried to be mindful of both health and environmental impact:

1. **Washing Produce:** I washed snap peas and spinach in a bowl of water with a low-flow faucet, minimizing water usage. Any vegetable trimmings went straight into my compost bin—a habit from fieldwork days that feels right at home.
2. **Fish Preparation:** After patting the tilapia fillets dry using a reusable kitchen cloth (no more paper towel waste for me!), I seasoned them gently with olive oil, fresh lemon juice, minced garlic, and cracked black pepper.
3. **Energy-Efficient Cooking:** I opted for my electric grill, which is more energy-efficient than the oven or stovetop. The tilapia cooked quickly—about three minutes per side—while I tossed snap peas and spinach in olive oil and sautéed them in a stainless-steel basket right on the grill.
4. **Plating & Reuse:** Lunch went onto my favorite reusable plates, with lemon wedges on the side. I saved the citrus peels for homemade natural cleaner, which feels like a small but meaningful step toward less waste.
5. **Additional Eco Actions:** I made sure to recycle all produce packaging and timed my cooking during off-peak energy hours.
