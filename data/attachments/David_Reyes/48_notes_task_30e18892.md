## 4. Expected Operational Outcomes and Technical Notes

### Anticipated Benefits to Fieldwork and Research

- **Boosted Propagation and Survival Rates:**  
  Implementation of Desert Roots Organics’ soil blend is expected to further improve seedling establishment and survival rates. This expectation is grounded in recent side-by-side trials, which demonstrated 88% seedling survival in conditions closely mimicking field environments—a marked improvement over previous industry-standard options.

- **Supporting Team Health and Morale:**  
  The decision to provide a selection of caffeine-free, organic herbal teas is based on team feedback and consideration of prolonged exposure to high summer temperatures. Observationally, these beverages help maintain hydration, support focus, and enhance overall morale during extended days in the field.

- **Streamlined, Sustainable Sample Management:**  
  Use of recyclable sampling bags and organic, latex-free gloves not only streamlines collection and processing of materials but reinforces our field commitment to sustainable research operations and minimizing environmental footprint.

- **Rigorous Quality Assurance:**  
  All materials undergo thorough batch testing and documentation, from soil composition verification to OMRI certification checks. This comprehensive quality control ensures repeatable research results and supports grant compliance requirements.
