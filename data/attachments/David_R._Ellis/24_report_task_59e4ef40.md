## Executive Summary

The April Financial Literacy Workshop was developed with a clear purpose: to empower individuals and communities with practical knowledge about managing money, making informed financial decisions, and participating in the broader conversation around economic policy. By combining interactive learning, hands-on activities, and accessible educational resources, we strive to address both fundamental concepts and advanced topics in personal finance.

We designed this workshop to welcome community members from all walks of life, including educators and policymakers. Our goal is to foster an inclusive environment where participants gain greater financial knowledge, develop positive habits such as budgeting and saving, improve their credit management skills, and confidently plan for their financial futures.

Outcomes we anticipate include:
- Increased confidence and skills in everyday financial management  
- Improved understanding of personal, family, and community financial issues  
- Enhanced resource sharing among educators for ongoing financial literacy outreach  
- Policy insights that help address local challenges in financial education  

All of our workshop materials reflect extensive review and direct input from stakeholders, ensuring every resource aligns with best practices for financial education for both youth and adults.

## Feedback Integration

Feedback has been integral to the development and refinement of our workshop. For example, Alicia Torres, one of our youth participants, suggested making our materials more visually engaging and requested breakout activities tailored to teenagers’ real-life experiences. She also highlighted the importance of using stories and examples that directly relate to teens. In response to Alicia's input:
- We redesigned youth handouts to prioritize clear layouts and colorful visuals  
- Interactive case studies were added to all youth sessions  
- Session 4 was completely revised to feature youth-focused breakout groups  

This collaborative process enabled us to make the workshop more responsive and relevant to all participants.

## Finalized Workshop Materials Overview

### Materials Overview

Our materials set is designed not just for transfer of information, but for active, hands-on learning. Every resource ties back to the workshop’s key objectives and is curated for both accessibility and effectiveness. The finalized materials include:

- A professionally formatted agenda with detailed session timings  
- Facilitator guides outlining session objectives and suggested facilitation scripts  
- Participant handouts, including easy-to-use budgeting templates, savings trackers, and plain-language credit summaries  
- Slide decks for each session, available in both PDF and PowerPoint formats  
- Youth-specific worksheets and interactive games, designed to engage and motivate  
- Case study packets that allow for small group work and scenario-based problem solving  
- Continuous improvement feedback forms, encouraging participant voices  
- A resource directory with further reading, post-workshop support contacts, and advocacy information  

These materials were carefully designed to:
- Build practical skills in budgeting, saving, and credit management  
- Promote engagement with real-life examples and role-play scenarios  
- Support differentiated instruction for adults and youth, making content accessible for all  
- Encourage active participation in discussions about financial policy and community advocacy
