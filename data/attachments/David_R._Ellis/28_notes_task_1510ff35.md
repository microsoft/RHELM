## Event Overview

The Family Gardening Day is designed to foster community engagement, provide an accessible outdoor experience for all ages, and offer educational enrichment through hands-on gardening activities. Building on feedback gathered at our recent Willow & Vine Café lunch, the program emphasizes inclusivity, collaboration, and celebration of our shared connection to nature.

---

## Event Details

### Date and Time

We are aiming for **Saturday, April 27, 2024**, which offers the best availability for families. If weather or scheduling conflicts arise, **Sunday, April 28, 2024** will serve as the backup date. Activities are scheduled to begin at 10:00 AM and wrap up around 2:00 PM.

### Location

**Willow Community Garden, Central Park District**

Chosen for its accessible facilities, the garden features:
- **Wheelchair-accessible paths** and rest area benches throughout
- A **shaded communal zone** ideal for group activities and lunch
- Accessible restrooms within a short distance (less than 100 meters)
- A **secure, fenced environment** suitable for children
- Ample parking, including marked disabled parking bays close to the garden gate

This setting allows all participants—regardless of mobility or age—to comfortably engage in the day’s activities.

## Supply Checklist

To ensure a welcoming and accessible experience for everyone, the following supplies will be prepared:

- **Gardening Gloves:** A range of sizes for children, youth, and adults; latex-free options included to accommodate allergies
- **Hand Tools:** Lightweight trowels, hand rakes, and small spades with ergonomic grips suitable for both younger participants and older adults
- **Raised Planters & Tabletop Stations:** Adjustable setups for participants who prefer not to kneel or have limited mobility
- **Watering Cans & Spray Bottles:** Easy-grip handles for all dexterity levels
- **Seed Packets:** Selection of easy-to-grow flowers, herbs, and seasonal vegetables to give everyone a role in the planting process
- **Potting Soil & Compost:** Stored in bins at accessible heights for easy scooping
- **Garden Mats or Kneelers:** Added comfort for those working closer to the ground
- **Sun Protection:** Sun hats, SPF sunscreen, and natural bug repellent available at the welcome table
- **Hydration:** Reusable water bottles for every guest and a clearly marked hydration station
- **Refreshments:** An array of snacks—fresh fruit, nut-free options, sandwiches (with vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free choices) as well as water and juice to suit all dietary needs and preferences
- **Hygiene Supplies:** Wet wipes and hand sanitizer available at activity areas
- **First Aid Kit:** Easily accessible in the main area
- **Name Labels & Schedule Printouts:** In large font and high-contrast for easy reading by all participants
- **Event Signage:** High-contrast directional signs with icons for easy navigation
- **Waste & Recycling Stations:** Clear labeling to support sustainable practices

## Key Outcomes from Willow & Vine Café Lunch

During our recent planning lunch at Willow & Vine Café, several essential principles were established to guide the event:

- **Inclusive Access:** Every detail—from tools to refreshments—will be intentionally selected to ensure comfort and engagement for all ages and abilities.
- **Clear Preferences:** Consensus was reached on April 27 as the preferred date and Willow Community Garden as the event venue, based on accessibility and suitability for families.
- **Transparent Communication:** Invitations will highlight available dietary and access accommodations, actively inviting input from families.
- **Community Spirit:** The event will open with a group circle, fostering a welcoming environment, and close with a group reflection to share stories and strengthen community ties.
- **Educational Enrichment:** Monica proposed that both children and adults engage in basic financial literacy by working together on simple budgeting tasks—for example, deciding how to spend a communal budget on seeds and supplies.
- **Shared Responsibility:** Setup, facilitation, and cleanup will be organized as group efforts, giving everyone a stake in the event’s success.
- **Next Steps:** Key tasks include confirming RSVPs, finalizing catering and equipment orders, and providing all participants with clear event details and defined roles.

## Educational and Community-Building Components

Building a strong sense of learning and connection is at the heart of Family Gardening Day:

- **Financial Literacy:** Each family or team will receive tokens to “spend” on various seed packets, making real-world budgeting decisions together. This hands-on activity will be followed by a conversation about cost, choice, and the value of shared planning.
- **Collaborative Decision-Making:** In the “Garden Vote,” every participant—regardless of age—gets an equal say in which plants to grow. This exercise is designed to ensure all voices are heard and foster collective decision-making.
- **Celebrating Diversity:** By incorporating participant feedback on food, music, activities, and scheduling, the event continues to evolve as a shared experience. Creating opportunities for everyone to contribute to the agenda lays the groundwork for an engaged, welcoming atmosphere.
