## Discussion Summary

### Room and Venue Setup

David initiated the conversation by outlining the seating arrangement. The team agreed on a U-shaped layout to encourage interaction among participants and to give everyone a clear line of sight to the presenter. Alicia confirmed the room can accommodate up to 30 guests, with extra chairs available if registrations exceed expectations.

Accessibility received focused attention. To ensure compliance with ADA standards, the team committed to keeping aisles wide and unobstructed and to providing designated areas for wheelchair users. Alicia proposed placing clear signage for accessible entryways and including directions to nearby accessible restrooms in the event welcome packet. The venue’s final room assignment remains unconfirmed; Alicia is working with the facilities team and expects confirmation by week’s end.

### AV and Technology Requirements

Alicia reviewed the list of required AV equipment: a projector, screen, laptop connections, wireless microphones, and speakers. Both she and David agreed to bring backup laptops, with presentations stored both digitally and on USB drives. Workshop Wi-Fi access will be provided for presenters and participants, with instructions distributed at registration.

Contingency plans were thoroughly discussed. Should any equipment malfunction, printed copies of all slides will be available for reference. Extra batteries for microphones will be on hand, and an on-site IT technician is being assigned to provide immediate support. If full technology failure occurs, the team will pivot to paper-based facilitation for interactive activities to ensure learning objectives are met.

### Workshop Materials and Handout Distribution

David and Alicia established the materials each participant will receive: the workshop agenda, a comprehensive financial literacy workbook, a resource list for future learning, and event evaluation forms. The handouts will be pre-assembled into packets, provided both in printed form upon arrival and digitally via email before the event. Additionally, QR codes will be available at registration for quick access to all digital materials. The agenda will be set out on each table prior to participant arrival, ensuring everyone is oriented from the beginning.

Alicia will oversee all print jobs, targeting completion by April 18, and delivery to the venue on April 19. She plans to coordinate with the venue's staff to streamline packet distribution during check-in.

### Contingency Planning

Adaptability was a central theme. A flexible time buffer is built into the workshop schedule to absorb any delays from technology glitches or participant needs. Alicia is in the process of reserving an alternate room, just in case of last-minute venue changes, with confirmation due by April 17.

David has prepared to adjust facilitation strategies based on final attendee numbers, ensuring group activities remain effective for both smaller and larger audiences. To accommodate unexpected changes, such as a facilitator absence, he will designate a backup and Alicia will draft a communication template for timely participant notification.
