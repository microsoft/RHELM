## Evidence-Based Best Practices for Workshop Delivery

Financial literacy education is most effective when it is participant-centered, practical, and adaptive to different contexts and audiences. Drawing from NEFE, Jump$tart Coalition, and OECD/INFE research, this workshop will incorporate the following strategies:

1. **Active, Skills-Based Learning:**  
   Hands-on activities, real-world case studies, and opportunities for practicing new skills are emphasized throughout all sessions. By engaging with realistic financial scenarios—such as budgeting for a household, choosing savings methods, or evaluating financial policies—participants can directly apply concepts and see their relevance.

2. **Consensus-Building and Collaborative Decision-Making:**  
   Breakout groups and live feedback (such as polls and voting cards) ensure that all participants have a voice in shaping discussions and outcomes. Facilitators will encourage dialogue and synthesize input from each group, with the goal of producing actionable takeaways for every segment.

3. **Robust Evaluation Process:**  
   Learning will be measured before, during, and after the workshop using surveys, quizzes, live group reflections, and peer review sessions. These tools will capture both quantitative and qualitative changes in participant understanding, allowing ongoing refinement of the workshop and its materials.

4. **Adapting Content for Specific Audiences:**  
   To maximize relevance, facilitators will adjust materials, scenarios, and complexity level for children, adults, and policymakers. For example, children will work with relatable scenarios like managing allowance money, while adults focus on budgeting for households or career changes, and policymakers tackle broader financial programs and legislative impacts.

5. **Transparent Implementation and Progress Tracking:**  
   Task assignments and regular agenda updates support accountability among organizers. Timely completion of action items ensures the workshop runs smoothly and that all participants benefit from a well-prepared program.
