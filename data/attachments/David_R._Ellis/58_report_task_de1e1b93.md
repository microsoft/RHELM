## Overview

Following the budgeting workshop held on June 27, 2024, the workshop team initiated a thorough revision of the slide deck to optimize learning outcomes and respond directly to participant feedback. This revision process was a true collaboration involving students, educators, policymakers, and visual artist Jasper. Each change was carefully considered and approved by a cross-functional stakeholder group, ensuring that updates were aligned with best practices in financial literacy education and mutually agreed upon. Jasper’s new artwork now features throughout the presentation, enhancing visual engagement and supporting diverse learning needs.

The following log provides a detailed account of every slide revision, the reasons behind these decisions, and their anticipated impact on workshop participants. Each update reflects our ongoing commitment to transparency, educational responsiveness, and the continuous improvement of our financial literacy programs.

## Revision Process and Collaborative Decision-Making

Revisions were implemented in chronological order, with each change discussed during follow-up meetings and in correspondence among stakeholders. Feedback was gathered from students during the workshop, educators in post-session reviews, and policymakers via consultation on best practices. Jasper, our visual artist, contributed fresh, inclusive imagery that brings scenarios to life and supports multimodal learning. The process ensured that every update was consensus-based—no changes were made without group agreement and explicit rationale.

This collaborative approach not only improved the workshop materials, but also fostered a culture of transparency and shared ownership among all participants. Stakeholder contributions are carefully documented, so every voice is acknowledged and each suggestion is accounted for in the final product.

---

## Alignment with Financial Literacy Education Standards

All revisions are grounded in established research and policy guidance in financial literacy education:

- **Inclusivity and Visual Engagement:** Jasper’s artwork was specifically created to resonate with participants from varied backgrounds, making budgeting concepts more accessible and relatable.
- **Active, Learner-Centered Participation:** Interactive infographics and editable examples encourage participants to engage directly with content and apply concepts to their own experiences.
- **Personalization and Local Relevance:** Customized examples and localized resources reflect the realities participants face, supporting tailored learning and practical implementation.
- **Authenticity and Realism:** Summarizing live discussion points and embedding real-world case studies enhances relevance and fosters critical reflection on complex financial concepts.
- **Continuous Assessment and Self-Reflection:** The new interactive quiz and feedback section allow participants not only to measure their progress but also to reflect on next steps in their financial education journey.
