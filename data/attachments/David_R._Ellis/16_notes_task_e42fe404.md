## Incident Details and Actions

**1. David's Smartphone (Orange Juice Spill)**  
The device was exposed to liquid when orange juice spilled onto it during the morning workflow. Immediate action was taken by powering down the phone to avoid electrical shorting and potential internal corrosion. Given the device holds essential business data, contacts, and serves as a medium for two-factor authentication, its loss would directly impede secure access to proprietary financial resources.

**Planned Actions:**  
- Perform a professional cleaning and assessment for internal damage within four hours of incident.
- Prioritize comprehensive backup of all critical apps and account credentials.
- If unrecoverable, provide David an immediately available loaner device to prevent business interruption.

**2. Office Desktop (Sluggish Performance After Update)**  
Following an overnight OS and application update, the primary office desktop exhibited markedly slower response times. This impacted the ability to generate client reports and delayed several urgent financial transactions.

**Actions Taken:**  
- Basic troubleshooting performed: unnecessary background tasks stopped and non-critical startup processes disabled.
- Full system diagnostic scheduled for 6:00 PM outside business hours to minimize workflow disruption.
- If lag persists, plan to revert the update or contact vendor support to expedite resolution.

**3. Consulting Laptop (Device Unbootable)**  
Attempts to start the laptop resulted in blank screens, with no response to safe mode initiation or power cycling. This immediate loss of functionality suspended the affected consultant’s ability to handle remote meetings and process client portfolios.

**Immediate Steps:**  
- Physical battery reset and memory checks performed without success.
- Device assigned to on-site IT for expedited hardware imaging and data retrieval.
- Temporary laptop prepared for redeployment to restore work continuity for the consultant.

**4. Mobile Tablet (Audiobook App Crashes)**  
The company-issued tablet, used for listening to industry updates during commutes and breaks, began crashing immediately after launching the designated audiobook application. Though this did not affect client operations directly, it did disrupt regular professional development routines.

**Remediation Steps:**  
- Uninstalled and reinstalled the application.
- System logs checked for OS compatibility issues or conflicting software updates.
- If the crash recurs, a support ticket will be opened with the app's developer, and alternative learning platforms will be considered.

## Preventive Measures

To reduce recurrence of these types of disruptions:

- Enforce strict use of spill-resistant covers or liquid barriers in all environments where devices are in use.
- Provide training reminders on routine data backup for all essential user devices.
- Clearly communicate the timing and scope of any planned system or application updates, and execute major updates outside business-critical hours whenever possible.
- Maintain a pool of ready-to-go loaner devices to minimize downtime for staff handling critical business functions.
- Regularly review device and application compatibility before approving or implementing upgrades.
