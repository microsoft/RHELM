## Meeting Discussion

### 1. Approval of Previous Minutes

The board reviewed and unanimously approved the minutes from the March 2024 meeting. No revisions were needed.

---

### 2. Review of Financial Literacy Initiatives

Ms. Ramirez began by outlining the district’s goal to strengthen financial literacy education across all schools, supporting objectives set out in the strategic plan focusing on student life skills and real-world readiness.  
David Miller provided a data-driven overview of successful financial literacy frameworks, referencing national standards and recent academic research. He highlighted the importance of starting financial instruction early and embedding it within the school’s broader educational vision.

Board members discussed the importance of aligning new initiatives with district priorities and ensuring all schools, regardless of size or resources, would benefit from the effort.

---

### 3. Integration into Core Curriculum (Grades 6–8)

David Miller presented a proposal to incorporate financial literacy themes into core subjects—primarily Mathematics and Social Studies—for grades 6 through 8. He explained how modules could naturally complement existing curricula, supporting practical skills and promoting cross-disciplinary learning.

Principal Carter addressed the logistical aspects of implementation, such as available instructional time, teacher training needs, and managing curriculum changes without disrupting existing academic programs. After comprehensive discussion, the board agreed to pilot the integration at Lincoln Middle School for the 2024–2025 academic year. The pilot will allow for phased expansion, enabling the district to refine its approach based on feedback and outcomes.

---

### 4. Staff Training for Financial Literacy Education

Ms. Ramirez reported on current professional development offerings and identified a need for more specialized, hands-on workshops focused on financial literacy pedagogy.  
The board assigned David Miller to work with Ms. Ramirez in co-designing training modules tailored to staff needs.  
Members reviewed the results of a recent staff survey which revealed strong interest, particularly in flexible training formats that offer interactive and on-demand content. Recognizing the value of ongoing support, the board discussed implementing regular feedback and coaching for staff during rollout.

---

### 5. Community Partnerships & Resource Allocation

Mr. Jon Gupta advocated for partnerships with local financial institutions and businesses to supplement classroom resources and create relevant student experiences such as workshops, field trips, and guest speakers.  
The board supported formalizing outreach initiatives with banks and nonprofits, aiming to secure resource donations, real-world learning opportunities, and professional mentorships for students.  
Paul White and Jon Gupta volunteered to lead the outreach team, developing a schedule for partnership invitations and follow-up.

---

### 6. Stakeholder Feedback

Ms. Julia Harris shared the results of recent outreach with students, parents, and teachers. Most stakeholders welcomed the planned curriculum changes and expressed optimism about equipping students with practical life skills. They requested clear communication regarding curriculum updates and opportunities to provide input as implementation unfolds.

To promote transparency and inclusive engagement, David Miller proposed a multi-channel communication plan, including virtual town halls, regular surveys, and informational updates. The board affirmed its commitment to ongoing stakeholder involvement and clear messaging throughout the process.

---

## Closing Summary

Dr. Susan Lee concluded by thanking all attendees and affirming shared commitment to advancing financial literacy across the district. The meeting achieved consensus on initiating curriculum changes, expanding staff development, building robust community partnerships, and maintaining open, transparent communication channels. The board emphasized the importance of data-driven progress review to ensure effective, scalable implementation.

---

### Alignment with District Financial Literacy Goals

The approved plans support the district’s overarching mission to equip students with essential financial skills for adult life. Through structured curriculum pilots, targeted staff training, inclusive engagement with families and students, and strategic partnerships, the board laid the groundwork for lasting, impactful change. Implementation efforts will be regularly monitored and adapted to ensure alignment with national benchmarks and responsiveness to local needs.

The next review will focus on early results from the pilot program and feedback from stakeholders, with the goal of continuous improvement and district-wide expansion.
