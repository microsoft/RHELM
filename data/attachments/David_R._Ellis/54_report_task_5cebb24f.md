## Eastwood Middle School Feedback & Implementation

### Feedback Highlights

Feedback from students and educators at Eastwood Middle School was overwhelmingly positive and provided clear direction for further improvement. Survey responses indicated that students felt more engaged when presented with interactive, real-life budgeting examples, and the inclusion of visual aides helped clarify savings concepts. There was also a notable increase in parent participation when family budgeting tools were sent home.

- **Workshop Satisfaction:** 92%  
- **Improvement in Budgeting Skills Assessment:** 30% increase post-workshop

### Changes Based on Feedback

In response, the team has increased the use of visual aids, drawing more extensively on Jasper’s artwork collection. Hands-on learning—such as role-playing real-life budgeting scenarios—has been expanded, and every session now concludes with family engagement materials that students can share at home. These adjustments reflect recognized best practices in financial education, particularly those highlighted by the *National Endowment for Financial Education*. Workshops now include more formative assessment checkpoints, ensuring that student progress can be monitored and support provided as needed.
