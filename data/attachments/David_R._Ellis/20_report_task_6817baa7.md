- **Low-Impact Aerobic Activities:**  
  Mr. Ellis is encouraged to continue walking and using the stationary bike. These activities foster joint healing, promote cardiovascular fitness, and minimize physical stress.

- **Flexibility Exercises:**  
  Moderately challenging flexibility routines should be introduced, targeting full joint range and strengthening supporting muscles. This approach helps restore motion and prevent future stiffness.

- **Balance Training:**  
  Simple balance activities, such as single-leg stands, are now integrated. Evidence supports their role in reducing fall risk and improving functional independence.

### Restrictions

- High-impact activities such as running and jumping remain off-limits for an additional two weeks to protect the healing joint and prevent undue stress.

- Resistance training beyond bodyweight exercises is not advised until further review and clearance.

- Plan an in-person follow-up in three weeks, contingent on continued progress.
