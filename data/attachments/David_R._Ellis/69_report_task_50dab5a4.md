## Spotlight: Family Contributions from Monica and Jasper

During our feedback session, Monica (representing parents) and Jasper (one of our youngest participants) brought forward practical, inspiring ideas that captured the spirit of family involvement.

**Monica’s Input**
Monica suggested hands-on workshops where families work together on budgeting exercises using realistic scenarios from daily life—grocery shopping, school fees, and saving for goals. She emphasized the need for activities that build confidence and are approachable for everyone, regardless of prior experience with finances.

Action: The pilot family budgeting workshop is scheduled for launch by July 28, with Monica actively involved in planning and resource preparation. Her insights are shaping scenarios and materials to ensure relevance and engagement across age groups.

**Jasper’s Input**
Jasper’s idea was to make financial lessons fun for children through “financial storytime.” By weaving financial principles into stories and adding visual aids and interactive group activities, he wants kids to develop a natural interest in these concepts.

Action: Scripts and materials for children’s storytime are being developed, with Jasper reviewing drafts and testing activities with his peers. We expect these resources to be ready by August 10.

Both Monica and Jasper are not only sharing feedback but are also directly involved in shaping our next initiatives—setting an example of active family participation.

## Expanding Engagement: Key Feedback Themes and Next Steps

The feedback table highlights the community’s priorities for increased clarity in roles within collaborative projects, more opportunities for youth to lead activities, and greater accessibility in resources for all ages. Parents and educators stressed the importance of resources that do not presume prior financial knowledge. Meanwhile, logistical improvements—including simpler registration and accessible venues—were underscored as essential for full participation.

Every comment is matched with a specific action step, and those responsible for each area have committed to concrete deadlines. The shared accountability builds trust and momentum, ensuring that suggestions translate into real results.
