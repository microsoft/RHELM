## Discussion Highlights

### Review of Workshop Materials

The meeting began with a comprehensive review of the financial literacy workshop packet. Dr. Greene noted that the materials were clear, logically organized, and integrated essential financial concepts such as budgeting, saving, and informed spending. He especially appreciated the inclusion of real-world examples, which made the content relatable and practical for participants. The flow of the packet was praised for guiding learners through foundational principles to more advanced strategies.

### Budgeting Activity: Recommendations for Enhancement

A significant portion of the discussion focused on the budgeting activity. Dr. Greene recommended moving away from a traditional “fill-in-the-blanks” worksheet format to a more dynamic, scenario-based design. Under this approach, participants would receive fictional profiles representing a variety of income levels, family situations, and sets of financial challenges. Working in small groups, they would construct budgets, make decisions on savings priorities, and reflect on the trade-offs inherent in financial planning.

This interactive format encourages participants to apply concepts in realistic scenarios rather than simply recalling information. Both attendees emphasized that experiential learning – where individuals actively engage with material through discussion and problem-solving – leads to deeper understanding and skill retention.

Dr. Greene referenced guidance from leading organizations in financial education, including the National Endowment for Financial Education and the Jump$tart Coalition, highlighting that scenario-based activities encourage critical thinking and practical decision-making. These methods are aligned with recommendations from the U.S. Department of Education and reflect recognized best practices such as collaborative learning, context-driven instruction, and guided reflection on choices.

### Agreement and Next Steps

David and Dr. Greene reached a consensus that transitioning to an interactive scenario-based budgeting activity would significantly strengthen the workshop, fostering greater engagement and more meaningful learning outcomes. This decision reflects a commitment to implementing up-to-date, evidence-based educational strategies.
