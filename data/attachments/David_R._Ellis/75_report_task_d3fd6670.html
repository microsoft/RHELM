Key Discussion Points

Webinar Objectives

We confirmed our commitment to expanding financial literacy for children and families, especially those in underserved communities. The team aims to offer practical, evidence-based education that not only builds lifelong financial skills but also strengthens community awareness and self-confidence in financial decision-making.

Target Audience

The primary audience consists of children aged 7–14 and their families. We also intend to reach adult caregivers, educators, and local policymakers, recognizing their role in modeling and supporting financial behaviors. Deep outreach efforts will ensure we include diverse participants, particularly those from non-English-speaking households.

Educational Goals

Our curriculum will focus on demystifying key financial concepts such as saving, budgeting, distinguishing needs from wants, and the value of giving. Lessons are designed to cultivate positive financial attitudes and self-efficacy, with clear, actionable steps and engaging, real-life scenarios that connect with everyday experiences. Special emphasis will be given to making abstract ideas tangible for children through relatable examples and interactive exercises.

Engagement Strategies

To foster active participation, we will incorporate gamified challenges and hands-on activities throughout the webinar. Ms. Ramirez described plans to use Jasper’s artwork as the centerpiece for our budgeting challenge, giving children and families a visually appealing way to apply concepts. Breakout rooms will facilitate teamwork and peer discussions, while storytelling and art projects offer opportunities for creative expression and contextual learning.

Evidence-Based Practices

Grounded in recent financial education research, the webinar will integrate experiential learning, opportunities for real-time feedback, and peer-to-peer knowledge sharing. We are aligning our methods with recommendations from established studies and local data, employing various instructional modalities—visual, auditory, and kinesthetic—to address different learning styles.

Equity and Accessibility

Ms. Ramirez underscored the necessity of making our event welcoming for all. In addition to English, all sessions and materials will be available in Spanish to better serve bilingual families. Recognizing digital access limitations among some households, we will offer printable resources and support simple navigation and device compatibility. Outreach will be tailored to minimize barriers and ensure inclusivity.

Building Partnerships and Promotional Strategy

Our outreach plan calls for active collaboration with local schools, libraries, and community organizations. By connecting with social media influencers and established youth advocates, Alicia Torres will lead efforts to broaden our promotional reach. The team will distribute downloadable flyers, coordinate targeted email campaigns, and utilize community boards to inform and invite participants.

Consensus-Building Moments

Several moments of agreement highlighted our shared priorities: a strong emphasis on inclusivity and family-centered learning; enthusiastic support for the interactive budgeting activity featuring Jasper’s artwork; and an ongoing commitment to stakeholder evaluation, ensuring our process remains responsive and evidence-driven.

Adaptation for Audience Segments

  • Children
    Lessons for children will use straightforward language and highly visual materials, interspersed with frequent interactive breaks and creative elements to maintain interest and support hands-on learning.

  • Adults/Caregivers
    Supplemental materials will be available for adults and caregivers, featuring advanced information and actionable tips for guiding children’s financial growth. These materials will include culturally relevant examples and community resource lists, ensuring that adult participants can both support and extend the lessons at home.

  • Policymakers
    For policymakers and community leaders, the team will prepare concise executive summaries, detailed community data analyses, and recommendations for scalable pilot programs. Impact metrics will be highlighted to facilitate informed decision-making and future support for similar educational efforts.

Additional Observations

The team maintained a clear focus on adapting content for a variety of literacy levels, cultural backgrounds, and access needs. Our ongoing collaboration with community advisors and education professionals ensures that all activities remain relevant, engaging, and effective. Moving forward, we will solicit expert feedback regarding accessibility solutions for families with disabilities and continue to monitor the latest financial literacy research to keep our approach current. Technical support staff will be consulted as we finalize the virtual delivery plan, emphasizing stability and user-friendly design.
