## Interpretation & Clinical Findings

Martin demonstrates progressive improvements in both measured performance and subjective well-being. Plank endurance has surpassed the target range, flexibility has increased beyond age-based standards, and minor lower back discomfort is steadily resolving. Physical examination corroborated cable digital logs showing consistent participation in scheduled activities, appropriate rest days, and absence of acute injury.

## Recommendations

1. **Core Stability Progression**
   - Begin advanced plank exercises, specifically single-leg lifts:
     - Target: 3 sets of 30 seconds per side, three times per week.
     - Gradually extend to 45 seconds per set within four weeks, if well-tolerated.

2. **Training Structure**
   - Continue alternating endurance-based cycling days with core strength training for optimal adaptation and fatigue management.
   - Uphold at least one complete rest day per week.

3. **Flexibility & Mobility**
   - Maintain dedicated stretching sessions, especially after workouts.
   - Strive for a sit-and-reach measurement of at least 33 cm, seeking an incremental gain of 2 cm from this current baseline within the next eight weeks.

4. **Activity Logging**
   - Continue comprehensive activity documentation using Garmin Connect.
   - Ensure all training sessions and recovery activities are properly tagged.
   - Enable app notifications to flag significant increases in fatigue or abrupt spikes in training load.

5. **Milestone Objectives**
   - Attain a standard plank hold of at least 60 seconds and leg-lift plank of at least 30 seconds, with subjective fatigue not exceeding 2/10 by next evaluation.
   - Keep subjective lumbar tightness at or below 2/10, with lumbar flexion consistently above 5 cm on Schober test.

6. **Recovery and Monitoring**
   - Conduct daily morning self-assessment of back symptoms (0–10 scale), emphasizing days following higher-intensity sessions.
   - Immediately report any escalation in pain (>4/10), sudden weakness, or range-of-motion decline.
