## 4. Logistics, Setup, and Hospitality

Creating the right environment is essential for a successful gathering. Here are detailed recommendations to ensure the evening runs smoothly:

**Location Options**
- **Home Setting:** Hosting at someone’s home, preferably with a spacious dining or living room table, is the most comfortable and allows for a relaxed atmosphere. I suggest reserving this space in advance to avoid any last-minute scrambles.
- **Office Common Space:** If after-hours access is permitted, the office’s common room can be comfortable and familiar. Please check any policies on food, drink, or guests before the event.
- **Local Café or Bistro:** Some cafés or bistros are board-game friendly and offer the right combination of lighting, space, and snacks. Before the night, I recommend confirming the venue’s policy on outside games and reserving a table, if possible.

**Food and Beverages**
- **Snacks:** To keep hands clean and the games in good condition, non-greasy finger foods are best—think pretzels, mixed nuts, veggie sticks, and cheese platters.
- **Main Fare:** If hunger’s likely to set in, simple and shareable takeout (pizza, wraps, or sandwiches) encourages casual dining without disrupting play.
- **Drinks:** Stock up on water, sparkling beverages, and tea. For those who enjoy it, wine or beer can add a celebratory touch—just check with the group for preferences.

**Online or Remote Participation (if needed)**
- If in-person attendance isn’t feasible for anyone, platforms like Board Game Arena or Tabletopia host digital versions of most recommended games. I’ll ensure everyone has the necessary accounts and check our internet connections beforehand. For a more social atmosphere, we can use video or voice chat via Zoom, Teams, or Discord throughout the evening.

**Final Preparations**
- Decide on the games ahead of time based on poll feedback.
- Review rules or quick reference sheets in advance, so we can jump straight into playing.
- Arrange seating and lighting—good visibility is essential for most games.
- Communicate the final plan—confirm the exact date, location, game list, and any food details—at least 48 hours ahead, to give everyone time to prepare.
