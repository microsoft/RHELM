## 3. New Additions and Updates

### Newly Acquired Games and Expansions

**Everdell: Mistwood**  
- *Publisher*: Starling Games  
- *Edition*: 1st Edition, 2024 (acquired August 2024)
- *Publisher Info*: [Starling Games – Mistwood](https://www.starling.games/everdell-expansions)
- *Release & Integration*: Released in North America May 2024. Fully compatible with all existing Everdell expansions, including Bellfaire and Spirecrest. The Mistwood expansion adds the "Nightweave" automated opponent, greatly expanding solo and cooperative play.
- *Notes*: Requires the base game v1.5 or newer. Officially updated rulebook (PDF, June 2024) clears up several card interactions; highly recommended for reference.

**Ark Nova: Marine Worlds**  
- *Publisher*: Feuerland Spiele  
- *Edition*: 1st English Edition (acquired July 2024)
- *Publisher Info*: [Feuerland Spiele – Marine Worlds](https://www.feuerland-spiele.de)
- *Release & Integration*: Released Q4 2023 in English. Expansion introduces “Sea Animal” icons but does not impact compatibility with previous printings. All components fit seamlessly with the 2024 reprint.
- *Notes*: The icon addition is straightforward; transition for players has been smooth.

### Updates on Existing Games

**Cascadia: Wildlife Expansion**  
- *Status*: Announced for late Q4 2024, preordered in August.
- *Integration*: Designed for all versions of Cascadia released since 2021. It brings new tiles and achievement mechanics, adding variety and depth for veteran players.
- *Anticipation*: Our group is looking forward to these new challenges, especially for solo play.

**Spirit Island: Jagged Earth**  
- *Rule Update*: Revised rulebook with errata published July 2024. PDF download is available on the Greater Than Games website. The update clarifies spirit interactions and fixes minor card timing issues.

### Additional Notes

- Rule sheets for all recent expansions have been printed and included in storage.
- Inventory is now fully checked and aligned with expansion compatibility notes provided by publishers.

## 5. Wishlist – Top Priorities for Future Acquisitions

Based on recent group discussions, interest trends, and what’s currently missing from our collection, I’ve re-evaluated and prioritized the wishlist as follows:

1. **Cascadia: Wildlife Expansion**  
   Highly anticipated by everyone—this will significantly expand gameplay variability and solo options, which we value for between-group sessions and new player engagement.

2. **Heat: Heavy Rain Expansion** (expected late 2024)  
   Adds weather dynamics and advanced track cards. This expansion has generated a lot of buzz, especially among our strategy-minded players who enjoy Heat’s replayability.

3. **Dune: Imperium – Uprising**  
   David and Elisabeth have both requested more deck-building options with an area control twist. This title is frequently discussed as filling that niche in our collection.

4. **Cartographers Map Pack Collection**  
   Modular map packs would add fresh content to our favorite filler/roll-and-write. They’re compact and ideal for shorter, casual sessions.

5. **Spirit Island: Nature Incarnate**  
   Our group gravitates toward deep cooperative games, and this recent expansion is at the top of several members’ personal lists.

Each wishlist item has been selected to add variety, fill genre gaps, or meet recurring specific requests from the group.
