## Technique Review and Observations

**Perceived Exertion (Borg Scale 6–20):** 13 (“Somewhat hard”)

### Technique Notes

- **Body Position:** Throughout most freestyle sets, body alignment remained streamlined. However, slight loss of form occurred during the final repetitions of the 6-beat kick drill, likely due to fatigue.
- **Breathing:** The new controlled breathing drill, involving inhaling every 5th stroke, showed an immediate effect on bilateral rhythm. While I maintained the pattern for most of each length, I found myself reverting to quicker breath-holding and shorter cycles towards the end of each rep when under more exertion. This will require ongoing adaptation.
- **Arm Mechanics:** During the catch-up drill, single-arm extensions were consistent, with high elbow entry on most passes. Notably, the final rep revealed a tendency for premature dropping of the forearm.
- **Kick:** The flutter kick held steady in timing, but fatigue led to a slight increase in knee bend, especially during the 6-beat kick series.
- **Pull Symmetry:** Pull buoy exercises highlighted a subtle right-arm dominance. I made a mental note to focus more on left/right balance in future sessions, ensuring symmetrical propulsion.
- **Hand Entry:** I noticed minor crossover with the left hand on entry during catch-up drills, reminding me to reinforce proper shoulder rotation and wide entry points on subsequent sessions.
