# Martin Keller Digital Board Game Inventory: Q3 2024 Update Report

## Inventory Overview

During the third quarter of 2024 (July 1 – September 30), my digital board game collection saw steady, thoughtfully-directed growth. The total catalogue expanded to 42 titles, representing an 8% increase compared to the previous quarter. This period was marked by a distinct focus on games that are more accessible and quick to set up, with a particular emphasis on titles that support solo play and offer engaging yet meditative experiences. The newly acquired games reflect an intentional move away from heavier, time-consuming strategy games in favor of flexible options—particularly those that work well for both solo sessions and two-player cooperative play.

I continued to see an increase in solo play activity, with solo sessions rising by 16%. Group play (three or more players) held steady in the absolute number of sessions but became a smaller proportion of total play, largely due to recurring scheduling conflicts within the group. Tile-laying and puzzle-based games dominated both wishlist additions and new acquisitions, which aligns not only with wider genre trends but also with my own evolving gameplay preferences over the past few months.

**Key Highlights for Q3:**
- **Inventory growth:** 3 new titles added (total now at 42)
- **Genre preferences:** Increased focus on puzzle and tile-laying mechanics, favoring lighter, adaptable games
- **Gameplay patterns:** Noticeable growth in solo sessions (+16%), with group play remaining steady in actual numbers but proportionally lower
- **Strategic adjustments:** Prioritized games with swift setup times and strong solo/duo modes to accommodate limited group availability
