## Course Overview

### Provider and Accreditation

The selected course is the Embedded Systems Professional Certificate (Advanced Level), offered through Carnegie Mellon University, a widely recognized leader in engineering education. The program is regionally accredited and ABET-aligned, ensuring that the curriculum meets the highest industry standards. Delivered fully online, it combines live virtual lectures, interactive labs, and hands-on projects designed to mirror real-world engineering scenarios.

- **Provider:** Carnegie Mellon University – Professional Education
- **Accreditation:** Regionally accredited; ABET-aligned
- **Format:** Remote learning, including live sessions and labs
- **Duration:** 12 weeks (3 hours of live sessions per week, plus 2 hours recommended for self-study and lab work)
- **Certification:** Advanced Professional Certificate in Embedded Systems

### Detailed Syllabus and Module Breakdown

The course consists of six sequential modules. Each is tailored to deliver both theoretical understanding and practical experience directly applicable to our projects:

| Module | Topic                                             | Format               | Duration  |
|--------|---------------------------------------------------|----------------------|-----------|
| 1      | Advanced Embedded Architectures                   | Virtual Lectures     | 2 weeks   |
| 2      | Real-Time System Design and RTOS Internals        | Lectures + Labs      | 2 weeks   |
| 3      | Embedded Networking & IoT Protocols               | Lectures + Labs      | 2 weeks   |
| 4      | Model-Based Systems Engineering (MBSE) for Embedded | Lectures + Project   | 2 weeks   |
| 5      | Secure Embedded System Design                     | Lectures + Labs      | 2 weeks   |
| 6      | Verification, Validation, and Test Automation     | Lectures + Labs      | 2 weeks   |

Each module includes structured assessments, hands-on exercises, and a capstone project focused on integrating all skill areas.
