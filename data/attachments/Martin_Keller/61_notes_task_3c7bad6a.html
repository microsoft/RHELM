My Weekly Shopping Checklist

Week of August 13, 2024
— Martin Keller

Personal Overview

Each week, I try to organize my shopping list so that everything I need—both for work and at home—is covered, especially when I can’t make it to the Wochenmarkt. Over the past year, I’ve found that a structured, prioritized list helps me avoid missing essentials and makes the whole process much less stressful. With my work as an embedded systems developer, I also have to account for some unique workplace needs, like blue-light filtering lenses and proper desk snacks. This week, I’ve added notes on sourcing sumac blend (still out of stock!) and a few reminders for professional gear that tends to fall through the cracks. Where possible, I’ve included suppliers I trust and listed alternatives if my usual choices aren’t available.

Finding Specialty Items: My Approach

Sumac Blend:
Sumac adds a lift to so many of my favorite dishes, but lately it’s been hard to find. My first stop will be Turkish or Lebanese delis around Frankfurt. If nothing turns up, I trust Spicebar.de and, in a pinch, Amazon.de for quality. Ordering two jars at once has saved me before, especially since unopened sumac keeps well.

Blue-light Filtering Lenses:
Since I rely on screens all day, having an extra pair of these is essential. I’ve had good luck with Gunnar and Cyxus in the past—their glasses really do help with eye strain. For quick turnarounds or fittings, Fielmann or Apollo are reliable local options, and if I’m ever in a hurry, places like MediaMarkt sometimes stock decent blue-light glasses.

Replacing the Wochenmarkt: My Game Plan for Key Essentials

Missing the Wochenmarkt always means a hit to freshness. For produce, eggs, bread, and cheese, I seek out the supermarkets’ organic sections or trusted bakeries. I pay attention to which suppliers do a good job so I can refine my list for next time, noting price, origin, and taste. Each week, small changes like this really help me keep my food quality up despite busy routines.

Staying Organized as a Professional

Since so much of my week revolves around focused desk work, I put effort into having a well-equipped, comfortable workspace—and good nutrition to back it up. The checklist above is more than just a list of things to buy; it’s a way to keep both my home and professional life running smoothly. By leaving plenty of space for notes, I can gradually fine-tune each area where something isn’t quite right—whether it’s desk comfort, running out of mineral water halfway through the week, or discovering a new favorite snack. These small optimizations add up, both in my work and my personal life.

My best tips for weekly shopping

  • I always review my checklist before heading out to make sure nothing is forgotten, paying special attention to high-priority items I can’t do without.
  • I jot down new observations—whether a supplier had excellent spinach, or I found a fantastic substitute for sumac—so my shopping gets smarter each week.
  • Reusable grocery bags live by the door now; I try hard to cut down plastic waste wherever possible.
  • Whenever possible, I combine trips: picking up snacks and supplements at DM, and personal care items at the same time to save both time and energy.
  • For anything I order online, I check delivery times and keep an extra on hand to avoid running out at inconvenient moments.
