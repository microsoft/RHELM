## 3. Preparation

I enjoy making dishes where the flavors come together quickly yet taste thoughtfully prepared. Here’s how I put the salad together:

**1. Washing & Chopping:**  
I started by rinsing the cherry tomatoes, cucumber, parsley, and basil under cool water—nothing beats that earthy scent of freshly washed herbs. The cherry tomatoes were sliced into halves and, when some were a bit larger, quarters. I peeled and finely sliced the red onion, aiming for slivers thin enough to mellow their bite in the salad. The cucumber was cut into half-moon slices—easy to eat and perfect for cradling the salad’s dressing. Lastly, I roughly chopped the parsley and basil, appreciating the burst of green aroma each time my knife hit the board.

**2. Mixing the Ingredients:**  
All the vegetables and herbs went into a large salad bowl. I gave them a gentle toss with my hands, enjoying the contrast of colors and textures as everything came together.

**3. Dressing and Seasoning:**  
I drizzled over my favorite olive oil and squeezed in the fresh lemon juice, the two combining to make a vibrant dressing. The sumac sprinkled on top added a ruby hue and an unexpected tart note—one of my favorite tricks for making salads pop. I crumbled the feta cheese over everything, then seasoned with a pinch of sea salt and a few twists of black pepper, making sure the flavors stayed balanced.

**4. Tossing & Resting:**  
The salad came together in just a few gentle tosses. Before serving, I let it rest at room temperature for about 5-7 minutes so the flavors could meld—the difference is subtle but worth it.

**5. Serving:**  
I served the salad as my main meal tonight. It worked perfectly on its own, though it would be easy to pair with some grilled chicken or chickpeas for extra protein.

*Prep time:* About 15 minutes  
*Difficulty level:* Easy  
*Kitchen note:* I chopped herbs and veggies while prepping other ingredients, which made everything flow smoothly and left me with minimal cleanup.

## 5. Reflections and Personal Notes

**How it tasted:**  
This salad tasted as bright and vibrant as it looked. The tomatoes and cucumbers brought a crisp freshness, the feta made each bite creamy and satisfying, and the fresh herbs tied it all together. The sumac, with its subtle citrusy tartness, really enlivened the entire dish—sometimes it’s the smallest additions that make the meal stand out.

**How it felt:**  
I finished dinner feeling genuinely satisfied but not weighed down, which is exactly what I needed after a nonstop day. There’s something soothing about knowing I’ve eaten plenty of vegetables and heart-healthy fats without overdoing it. It was substantial enough as a solo dinner, but I know it would work just as well as a generous side if I wanted to build out a bigger spread.

**Ease and efficiency:**  
I appreciated how effortless this meal was to prepare. It slotted perfectly into a hectic evening, taking no more than 20 minutes from start to finish. Cleanup was minimal, and the process was almost meditative—a welcome break in my day. Sometimes I prep extra herbs or slice extra veggies for quicker meals later in the week.

**Adaptability:**  
This recipe’s beauty comes from its flexibility. On another night when I want a bigger protein boost, I’d happily add grilled chicken, chickpeas, or even a handful of quinoa. But as written, I found this meal perfectly balanced for a light yet filling dinner.
