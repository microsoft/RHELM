Discussion Summary

1. Standing Action Items

Julia opened the meeting with a review of action items from the previous session. Most of these have been completed; however, Martin noted that the hardware procurement update remains open pending confirmation from the supplier.

2. Project Timeline & Milestones

The team remains on track for the upcoming major firmware release, targeted for the July sprint. All critical dependencies for this milestone were discussed. There was consensus that the hardware delivery date is still a significant risk point, so this remains under close monitoring. Julia emphasized the importance of contingency planning in case of further delays.

3. Technical Progress & Blockers

Martin shared that integration tests for the new communication protocol have been completed successfully, contributing to overall system stability. Mehmet described an ongoing investigation into a timing issue with real-time sensor polling that surfaced during functional testing. This issue could impact the responsiveness of the system if not addressed. Due to ongoing supply chain delays, physical hardware testing could not begin as initially planned. In response, the group explored alternative approaches and agreed to expand the use of emulated hardware environments to allow development and verification to proceed without waiting for hardware arrivals.

4. Metrics Review

Julia reported that code coverage has increased from 72% to 80% since the previous sync meeting, with the team aiming to reach 85% coverage by the next sprint. Additionally, the unresolved issue backlog has been reduced by 15%. Both improvements reflect sustained progress and focus from the development team.

5. Hardware Testing Approach

The group formally agreed to use emulated hardware for all preliminary verification activities while awaiting the official hardware delivery. This ensures that critical test cases continue to progress and helps maintain momentum on core project threads.

6. Assignments & Alignment

Action items were redistributed to keep all team members focused on immediate priorities. Particular attention is being given to unblock issues that could affect the upcoming release milestones.

7. Next Steps and Meeting Preparation

The team established the main priorities for the coming week: resolving the sensor polling issue, finalizing the emulation test scripts, confirming the hardware procurement timeline, and updating project risk documentation. Tasks were assigned accordingly, and all participants are expected to update relevant Jira tickets prior to the next sync.
