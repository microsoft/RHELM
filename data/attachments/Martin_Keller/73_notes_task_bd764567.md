## Overview

This log is designed to support software engineers—like myself—who are balancing the challenges of shoulder rehabilitation with the mental demands of daily coding and technical work. The structure makes it easy to integrate these health insights into my regular workflow, helping me track progress, recognize patterns, and maintain healthy habits over the long term.

## 6. Visualization Guidance

To get deeper insights, I regularly visualize my data:

- **Line Graphs:**  
  - Track sleep duration and quality to see if better rest supports faster recovery or improved focus.
  - Shoulder pain and range of motion over time show my rehabilitation trajectory—where progress is steady, or where I need to adjust.
  - Cognitive stamina and task performance help monitor mental resilience, helpful for workload planning.
- **Bar Charts:**  
  - Compare exercise intensity and types by week to prevent overtraining or missing essential rehab movements.
  - See how typing/mouse comfort changes in relation to physical therapy, highlighting successes or areas of concern.

These visuals not only help me but also provide useful material to discuss with my physiotherapist or occupational health specialist.
