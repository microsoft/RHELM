## Analysis

### Session Disruption

The primary obstacle today was uncontrollable external construction noise. High-decibel auditory disturbances disrupted my concentration, elevated my stress levels, and negatively affected motor coordination. For routines focused on core control and joint mobility, a calm environment isn’t a luxury—it's a necessity. Continuing under those conditions would have risked poor form or even injury.

### Right Shoulder Twinge

The mild shoulder pain during CARs most likely stemmed from pre-existing muscle tightness or slight tendon irritation in the rotator cuff, exacerbated by quickly moving into an overhead range without sufficient dynamic warm-up. The added environmental stress may also have reduced movement quality. There was no evident loss of strength or function, but I’ll take this as a reminder to better prepare the shoulder complex before starting demanding mobility work—especially on days when distractions are present.
