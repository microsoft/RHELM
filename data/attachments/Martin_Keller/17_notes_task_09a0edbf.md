## Home Setup: Creating a Welcoming Space

I wanted everyone to feel comfortable settling in—good food is important, but ambiance and logistics are what really set the tone for the night.

### Cleaning, Setting Up and Double-Checking

**Kitchen:**  
- Swept and mopped, wiped counters, cleaned the sink, and ran the dishwasher. Bin emptied, and I paid extra attention to sanitizing prep tools since we’d be snacking and playing simultaneously.

**Dining/Game Area:**  
- Extended the dining table, wiped everything down, sanitized chairs, and scouted for a few extra stools and benches. Placed snacks at arm’s reach from game boards.

**Living Room:**  
- Quick vacuum and dust. Moved a few things to open up floor space—gave everyone room to move or lounge between games.

**Seating:**  
Confirmed guest count and ensured seating for all eight, with a cushion-and-stool backup plan just in case. Made sure there was a clear path to food and the game table—nobody needs to navigate an obstacle course for hummus.

**Pre-Event Checks:**  
- Lighting: Replaced a bulb and added a standing lamp for the evening.
- Music: Synced up a mellow Spotify playlist to my Bluetooth speaker—kept the volume low enough for game banter.
- Ventilation: Opened the windows for a quick air-out so the apartment felt fresh right from the start.

## Reflections: What Worked, What to Improve

### Highlights
- Shopping was painless—Alnatura and the Turkish market had everything on my list, so no last-minute stress.
- The snack platters, especially the hummus and feta, were real crowd-pleasers—lots of compliments (and nothing left over).
- The apartment felt cozy and uncluttered, and flexible seating worked really well as guests drifted in and out.
- Both Ark Nova and Revive got played, and I was able to explain the basics clearly enough for newcomers to enjoy.
- The mellow playlist and decent lighting had everyone relaxed and chatting.

### Lessons Learned & Room for Improvement
- My hummus came out spicier than I’d planned—next time, I’ll make a mild and a spicy batch so everyone’s happy.
- I ran low on non-alcoholic drinks earlier than expected. Clearly, more herbal teas, fruit spritzers, and perhaps some non-alcoholic beer would be a good idea.
- The explanation for Ark Nova dragged on a bit for new players. I’m going to prepare quick rule cards or printouts for these heavier games next time.
- A couple of guests (Jana and Emil) had to leave early. Next time, I’ll check if an earlier start time would work better for more people.
- We almost ran out of napkins and plates. I’ll stock up extra and prepare a replenishment tray for snacks and cutlery ahead of time.

### Action Items for Next Board Game Night
- Test and split the hummus recipe for spice preference before the event.
- Add more non-alcoholic beverage options, including teas and mocktails.
- Make print-friendly, one-page rule summaries for the complex games.
- Get everyone’s preferred arrival time early and aim for better overlap.
- Prepare an “extras box” with napkins, plates, serving utensils, and snack refills within easy reach.
