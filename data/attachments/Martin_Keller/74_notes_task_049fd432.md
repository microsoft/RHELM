## Subjective Assessment

This session reflected steady progress in both range of motion and shoulder control. In particular, I was able to achieve greater external rotation compared to earlier workouts, though the anterior shoulder tightness at maximal range remained noticeable. Pain peaked during end-range flexion, but overall remained within tolerable limits that did not impair my ability to complete exercises. Throughout the routine, I experienced only mild muscular fatigue, and did not need to break due to discomfort.

- **Shoulder Mobility:** 8/10 – I can now reach overhead shelves and cabinets, though I still feel a slight restriction with full flexion.
- **Shoulder Stability:** 7/10 – Most movements felt stable and secure; I only sensed some transient instability when my arm was fully abducted.
- **Shoulder Discomfort/Pain:** Average 3/10 – Discomfort was mostly limited to the final few degrees of flexion and abduction, and did not require modification of the exercises.

Overall, I felt noticeably more confident in daily tasks—such as reaching while cooking or carrying a light bag—without the apprehension or pain from previous weeks. My plan is to transition to a green (medium resistance) band in the coming sessions, as current loads are becoming increasingly manageable.
