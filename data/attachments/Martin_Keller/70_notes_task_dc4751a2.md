### 2.1 Activity Instructions and Technical Notes

#### Cycling

- **Monday (30 min steady, RPE 4–5):**
  - Maintain a moderate, steady pace. You should be able to hold a conversation, but notice deeper breathing.
  - Use a stationary bike or flat/moderate terrain outdoors.
  - Focus on smooth pedaling and relaxed posture—avoid upper body tension.

- **Wednesday (Interval Session, 5 sets of 3 min RPE 6 / 2 min RPE 2):**
  - Warm up for 5 minutes at easy pace.
  - Alternate 3 minutes of brisk cycling (RPE 6 — breathing hard, speaking only short phrases) with 2 minutes gentle spin (RPE 2).
  - Complete 5 cycles, then cool down for 5 minutes.

- **Friday (40 min easy, RPE 4):**
  - Aim for a continuous, easy ride focusing on consistency and endurance, keeping intensity moderate and comfortable.

#### Core Work

- **Tuesday & Thursday (15 min circuit; progression on Thursday):**
  - Complete two rounds of:
    - Bird-dog (10 reps per side)
    - Side plank (30 seconds per side)
    - Dead bug (10 reps per side)
    - Glute bridge (12 reps)
  - Rest 30 seconds between exercises.
  - On Thursday, add a third circuit or extend plank and bridge holds by 10 seconds each to progress.
  - Keep movements slow and controlled, focusing on core engagement and spinal alignment.

#### Resistance Band Exercises

- **Wednesday (20 min introductory full-body routine):**
  - Start with low resistance (yellow/orange band).
  - Complete 1 set each:
    - Band row (12 reps)
    - Chest press (12 reps)
    - Squat with band (12 reps)
    - Band pull-apart (15 reps)
    - Band shoulder external rotation (10 reps per arm)
  - Rest 1–2 minutes between exercises, emphasizing correct form.

- **Saturday (20 min progression):**
  - Increase to 2 sets of each exercise if prior session felt manageable.
  - Progress to the next resistance band (red/green) or add 2 repetitions per set as tolerated, maintaining strict form and pain-free movement.

#### Swimming

- **Explicitly not permitted** on any day. Medical clearance is required before resuming any aquatic activity.

#### Rest

- **Thursday:** Prioritize rest. A gentle, optional 30-minute walk is allowed but keep the pace light (RPE 3 or below).
- **Sunday:** Complete rest day. Light stretching or easy leisurely movement is optional; focus on recovery.

## 3. Personal Tracking: Observations and Metrics

Consistent self-monitoring is key to effective training and safe progression. Martin should log daily insights and weekly summaries to facilitate ongoing adjustment.

### 3.1 Daily Log (after each session)
- **Perceived exertion (RPE 0–10):** Self-rating of intensity
- **Discomfort or pain (details):** Note any unusual symptoms, location, or persistence
- **Enjoyment/motivation (1–5):** Overall satisfaction with session
- **Technique notes:** Thoughts on exercise form and movement quality
- **Progress indicators:** Resistance band color, sets, reps, changes in intensity
- **Sleep quality (1–5, previous night):** For insight into overall recovery
- **Additional comments:** Energy, mood, appetite, or any unusual reactions

### 3.2 Weekly Reflection (end of week)
- **Largest improvement:** Highlight progress or new achievements
- **Challenges/setbacks:** Note any difficulties or issues encountered
- **Adjustments for next week:** Consider tweaks to duration, intensity, or type of activity
- **General health notes:** Weight, blood pressure, or other observations if available

## 4. Summary of Dr. Lang’s Clinical Recommendations

### Action Plan

- **Implement a varied, structured weekly program** focusing on low-impact cardio (cycling), core stabilization, and progressively challenging resistance band exercises.
- **Alternate between cycling, core, and resistance days,** introducing new movements gradually to support musculoskeletal health, prevent injury, and enhance function for desk-based work.
- **Adhere strictly to the swimming prohibition** due to medical necessity, pending explicit clearance.
- **Integrate adequate rest and recovery,** ensuring balanced adaptation and minimizing injury risk.

### Safety and Progression Guidelines

- **Stop any exercise immediately** if you experience pain, significant fatigue, or symptoms such as dizziness or chest discomfort. Seek medical evaluation if symptoms persist.
- **Use the RPE scale** as a practical measure for controlling workout intensity. RPE 2–5 represents light to moderate efforts; only brief intervals should reach RPE 6.
- **Build up resistance gradually** with bands: only move to higher resistance, extra reps, or sets if you can maintain correct form and remain pain-free.
- **Maintain strict attention to movement quality**—avoid rapid or uncontrolled motions at all times.
- **Always include a thorough warm-up and cool-down** (at least 5 minutes of gentle movement before and after each session).
- **Stay hydrated** and observe any signs of overexertion.

### Rationale for the Swimming Restriction

Swimming remains off-limits due to important medical considerations. Common reasons for this restriction include:
- Recent injury, surgical recovery, or concerns about healing incisions or skin integrity
- Prevention of infection or protection of wounds/medical devices
- Pending medical evaluation related to cardiovascular or respiratory function

This approach is designed to balance safety with the benefits of exercise for sedentary professionals and to minimize risk during recovery or ongoing assessment.

### General Recommendations

- Aim for a minimum of two complete rest days weekly.
- Emphasize correct technique and controlled movement over speed or volume.
- Personalize the routine based on your weekly feedback, adapting activities in consultation with your healthcare provider as necessary.
