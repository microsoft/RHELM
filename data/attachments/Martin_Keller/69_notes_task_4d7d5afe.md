## 3. Preparation Steps

The workflow for this salad is designed for precision but with enough flexibility for kitchen realities. Here’s how I prepared it:

1. **Ingredient Preparation [T0]:**  
   I started by patting the halloumi dry using kitchen paper and slicing it into even 1.5 cm-thick slabs—each weighing about 50 grams. Cherry tomatoes were halved. I diced the cucumber into cubes around 1–1.5 cm and thinly sliced the red onion into rings (about 2 mm thick). After washing and spinning the salad greens, I set them aside to dry completely. Finally, I finely chopped the mint leaves so their flavor would mix well throughout the dish.

2. **Spice Blend Rationing [T+5 min]:**  
   For seasoning, I mixed 4 grams of Frau Bauer’s Mediterranean spice blend with 1 gram of sumac in a shallow dish—this would become the base spice for grilling. The remaining spice blend (2 grams) and sumac (1 gram) were reserved for garnishing the salad at the end, keeping their aroma bright and fresh.

3. **Grilling the Halloumi [T+10 min]:**  
   I preheated my grill to about 225°C (using a thermometer to check), brushed the halloumi slices with 10 milliliters of olive oil, and coated them evenly with the spice and sumac mixture. Grilling each slice for 2 to 2.5 minutes per side resulted in beautifully golden cheese with a crisp exterior and soft, creamy center. I checked the internal temperature with a probe—it reached about 55°C, which for halloumi is perfect for maintaining that signature squeak without turning rubbery. After grilling, the cheese needs to rest for about two minutes—this stabilizes the temperature and keeps the texture right.

4. **Vegetable Assembly [T+15 min]:**  
   While the halloumi rested, I combined the salad greens, tomatoes, cucumber, onion, and mint in a large bowl. To dress the salad, I added the remaining olive oil (30 milliliters), all the lemon juice, sea salt (2 grams), and cracked black pepper (1 gram). I tossed everything gently, making sure not to crush the delicate greens.

5. **Plating [T+20 min]:**  
   I arranged the salad on chilled plates and laid the warm, grilled halloumi slices on top. Then, I evenly sprinkled the reserved Frau Bauer’s spice blend and sumac across the top. This touch gave the dish a vibrant color and an aromatic lift.

6. **Final Check & Serving [T+25 min]:**  
   For an extra bit of richness, I sometimes drizzle on a touch more olive oil, up to 5 milliliters, especially if the salad looks dry. The dish is best served immediately while the halloumi is still warm.

**Active preparation and cooking time:** About 30 minutes from start to finish.

## 4. Special Notes

### 4.1 Sumac  
Sumac is key to this salad’s fresh, citric edge. The tangy, almost lemon-like quality balances the salty richness of the halloumi. I source my sumac as a finely ground powder from reputable importers specializing in Mediterranean ingredients (Kalustyan’s and several local specialty shops have never failed me). I learned over time that sumac’s aroma is most vibrant when added after cooking—the heat tends to dull its brightness, so I use it primarily for garnishing at the end rather than during grilling or in dressings.

### 4.2 Frau Bauer’s Mediterranean Spice Blend  
This proprietary blend, batch 2024A from Frau Bauer’s Spice Company, is a staple in my kitchen, especially for grilled vegetables and cheese. The mixture includes oregano, thyme, marjoram, rosemary, savory, a hint of chili, and dried citrus peel—all expertly balanced but with the specifics kept secret by the makers. I appreciate how it brings layered herbal notes and just a touch of heat, perfectly complementing the halloumi’s savory profile. For others trying this recipe, a high-quality Mediterranean herb blend without added salt serves as a reliable substitute (same proportions).

### 4.3 Ingredient Precision & Sourcing  
All ingredient weights were measured using a calibrated digital scale (1-gram precision), which is especially important for consistency in both professional kitchens and reproducible home cooking. I source all fresh produce within 36 hours of use through established suppliers, ensuring peak freshness and flavor. ## 5. Outcome and Flavor Notes

After assembly, the salad delivered exactly what I wanted: a satisfying mix of textures and flavors. Each bite combined the warm, creamy texture of grilled halloumi with crisp, cool greens and the brightness of fresh lemon and herbs. The spice blend imparted a complex herbal aroma that was both comforting and stimulating, while the extra sumac garnish amplified the dish’s freshness and visual appeal with its bold red flecks.

The finished plate looked lively—golden-brown halloumi slices, colorful vegetables, vivid green herbs, and the unmistakable reddish-purple of sumac. I served it immediately; halloumi always loses something when it cools. I found the balance of salt, tang, and herbal notes spot-on for a light meal or shareable starter.

## 6. Adjustments and Rationale

A few refinements were made from previous trials:

- **Increased Sumac:** I added an extra 0.5 gram of sumac for garnish, boosting the tart aroma which I felt was lacking before.
- **Tuned Spice Blend Quantity:** Initially, I tried 8 grams of spice blend in total, but it overpowered the halloumi’s natural flavor. Reducing to 6 grams allowed the cheese and other ingredients to shine while still providing herbal depth.
- **Thicker Halloumi Slices:** Bumping up the slice thickness from 1 cm to 1.5 cm meant fewer burnt edges and a more enjoyable bite—less rubbery, more creamy.
- **Optimal Grilling Temperature:** After several runs, I fixed the grilling temperature at 225°C. At this setting, the cheese browned perfectly without losing moisture.
- **Lowered Salad Salt:** With halloumi itself being quite salty, I reduced the addition to 2 grams for the salad; this was confirmed as ideal by everyone who sampled the dish.
