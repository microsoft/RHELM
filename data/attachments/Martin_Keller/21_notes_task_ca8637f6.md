## 1. Introduction and Trip Overview

This document outlines the comprehensive plan for a multi-day cycling tour across the Black Forest, tailored for Martin Keller and Lukas. Our route follows a classic north-to-south traverse, starting in Baden-Baden in the northern foothills and concluding in Waldshut-Tiengen on the Rhine.

**Key Details:**
- **Distance:** Approx. 350 km  
- **Total Elevation Gain:** 5,500–6,000 m  
- **Trip Duration:** 4–5 days (daily average: 70–90 km with climbs up to 1,200 m)
- **Key Route:**  
  Baden-Baden → Forbach → Freudenstadt → Triberg (including waterfall detour) → Titisee-Neustadt → Waldshut-Tiengen

The journey is designed to showcase the Black Forest’s diverse terrain—thick woods, panoramic ridges, deep valleys, and famous waterfalls. The itinerary combines official cycling routes with handpicked detours for scenery and local culture. Expect a mix of surfaces, including paved roads, forest tracks, and sections of gravel.

### Highlights Along the Way

- Ascent of the Hornisgrinde, the highest peak in the North Black Forest
- Classic passes like Kniebis and the high plateau stretches
- The dramatic Triberg waterfalls and the renowned Black Forest Railway trail
- Panoramic views from Brend and lakeside relaxation at Titisee
- The wild canyons of the Wutach Gorge, ending with a long descent to the Rhine

For navigation and updates, we rely on established GPS tracks and official tourism resources; details below include references for digital and printable maps.

**Special Notes:**
- **Triberg Waterfalls:** Leave main track in Triberg for a marked detour (add 2–3 km roundtrip, 1–1.5 h including sightseeing, photos, snack stops). The waterfalls are a top Black Forest highlight and are best visited during daylight hours for safety and optimal views.
- **Brend Panoramic Loop:** After leaving Furtwangen, an optional 5 km side loop offers broad panoramic vistas across the southern valleys. This section includes steep ascents and rapid descents; suitable for those seeking an extra challenge and spectacular photography spots.
- **Wutach Gorge:** Trail conditions can change quickly with weather. Always check [Wutachschlucht Trail Info](https://www.wutachschlucht.info/) close to departure for any closures due to flooding or maintenance.

Each segment is designed to balance scenic appeal, adequate rest points, and manageable daily targets considering sustained climbs.

## 4. Insights from Past Tours: Avoiding Common Pitfalls

Analyzing lessons from prior cycling experience, we incorporate preventive measures to enhance safety, navigation reliability, and comfort on this trip.

| Date    | Incident                        | Analysis & Cause                | Immediate Response      | Steps for Future Tours                                                            |
|---------|---------------------------------|---------------------------------|------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 2022-07 | Rear tire blowout (Feldberg descent) | Overheated rim, worn tire          | On-the-spot patch, roadside tire swap | Pre-ride tire assessment, replace any tire with >4,000 km use, inspect rims, pack spare tube & kit |
| 2022-09 | Missed trail fork (navigation error) | Incomplete GPS load & missing map  | Backtracked 5 km, time lost           | Always load complete GPX files, dual navigation (GPS + paper), double-check waymarks, redundant phone backup |
| 2023-04 | Nightfall before reaching town   | Underestimating distance/time      | Used bike lights, phoned hotel         | Conservative day planning, built-in time buffer, always carry lights, sunset alarm |
| 2023-08 | Thunderstorm on route            | Unchecked weather, no rain gear    | Shelter, waited out storm              | Check daily forecasts, always carry compact rain jacket, plan alternate shelter points|

**Key Takeaways:**  
Consistent technical checks, split navigation responsibilities, and always building in a time buffer have repeatedly prevented repeat incidents. These routines remain central to our planning for this tour.
