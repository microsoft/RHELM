## Visiting Maybachufer Market

Maybachufer (“Türkischer Markt”) is a lively, multicultural spot along the Landwehr Canal in Neukölln. On any given market day, there’s a blend of long-time locals, new Berliners, and tourists—everyone weaving between colorful stalls loaded with fruits, vegetables, baked goods, and specialty products. Bread and produce are the stars, but you’ll also find cheese, spices, and preserves. On this Saturday, the market was busy but relaxed, with the usual hum of chatter and the occasional music in the background.

I arrived not too early, wanting to avoid the very first rush but also get there before the best items were gone. I didn’t write down the exact time but it was late morning, with the sun already warming the cobblestones.

### A Few Observations

The asparagus and elderflower syrup from Frau Lehmann always stand out. Her asparagus is typically freshly picked, with a subtle sweetness and tender stalks, perfect for salads or a quick pan-fry. The elderflower syrup, a classic homemade touch in Berlin, is fragrant and delicate—wonderful mixed with sparkling water after a morning at the market.

For the veggies and bread, I made my way through the main produce avenues, choosing bright, crunchy radishes and deep-green spinach leaves. There’s no shortage of quality, but the selection and presentation vary by stall, so I tend to follow my instincts and what looks best in-person. Rye bread from this market has a hearty crust and robust flavor, making it a staple in my kitchen throughout the week.
