## 2. Closer Look: Unexpected Bank Fee and Resolution Steps

### Fee Overview

- **Date Charged:** August 3, 2024  
- **Description:** Account Maintenance Fee (unexpected) – BankX  
- **Amount:** €1.20  
- **Account Impacted:** My main operational checking account at BankX.  

According to BankX policy, this type of maintenance fee only applies if I don’t receive at least €700 in qualifying monthly deposits or if there are unusual account patterns. Reviewing statements from June and July, I noticed I was not charged any fees, which fits—my salary typically arrives early each month, and nothing else changed on my end.

This month, however, I was surprised by the €1.20 deduction on August 3, seen in my account summary with the usual maintenance fee label, yet with no forewarning or policy change notification.

#### Possible Causes, Based on BankX Terms:

- **Missed Deposit Threshold:** Perhaps my August deposit fell just short of the €700 minimum, possibly due to a salary payment coming in a day late or a smaller reimbursement.
- **Bank Technical Issue:** BankX has circulated notices about occasional glitches during online banking upgrades. It’s possible the fee was misapplied by their system.
- **Unusual Account Movements:** While I don’t recall any strange transactions, sometimes external project payments or advocacy-related inflows can inadvertently flag my account for review and lead to a one-off fee.
