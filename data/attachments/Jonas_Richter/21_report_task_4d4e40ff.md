## 3. Detailed Discussion Summaries

### 1. Finalization of Workshop Agenda

We began by reviewing recent feedback from both German and Polish collaborators. Dr. Nowak’s prior experience with similar bilateral workshops proved invaluable, especially in identifying the optimal timing and structure for intercultural exchange sessions. The consensus was to strike a thoughtful balance between technical learning, policy roundtables, and informal networking to encourage both knowledge sharing and relationship building among participants.

There are still two guest speaker slots yet to be confirmed. To address this, we designed the draft agenda with flexibility to accommodate late changes, ensuring minimal disruption if adjustments are required. Feedback from the Polish partner schools is expected by June 18, and we agreed to implement their input promptly upon receipt. In developing the agenda, we also referenced the 2023 workshop documentation to ensure continuity with previous successful formats.

### 2. Detailed Travel Planning for Polish Student Group

**Legal and Compliance Requirements:**  
We confirmed the need for parental consent forms, fully aligned with German youth travel legislation (see Child Protection Act: [Bundesministerium für Familie, Senioren, Frauen und Jugend](https://www.bmfsfj.de/bmfsfj/service/gesetze/kinderschutzgesetz-86214)). We also verified that a visa waiver applies for short-term educational visits under the current Schengen regulations between Poland and Germany.

**Transportation and Accommodation:**  
Train travel via Deutsche Bahn was selected for reliability and convenience. Group seat reservations are in progress to keep the travel party together, with a particular focus on accessibility and safety. Accommodation has been secured at a partner youth hostel familiar with international student groups. We established an emergency contact protocol, designating primary and secondary points of contact, and clarified expectations with hostel management regarding night-time supervision and medical contingencies.

**Safety and Risk Management:**  
Staying up-to-date with official travel advisories from the [German Foreign Office](https://www.auswaertiges-amt.de/de/ReiseUndSicherheit) was deemed essential, especially regarding any unforeseen risks related to health or security. We reiterated the importance of COVID-19 precautions and recommended all students check their vaccination status prior to departure. To foster a proactive risk management culture, risk assessment templates will be distributed to group leaders; submissions are required by June 20 to allow for prompt review and, if needed, supplementary planning.

### 3. Coordination of Logistics Summary Email to Frau Becker

Clear and consistent communication with administration is crucial for smooth implementation. Jonas will take the lead in drafting the summary email, with Dr. Nowak reviewing and refining the content to ensure all critical details are included and phrased clearly. The email package will include the finalized travel plan, the updated workshop schedule, emergency contacts, and digital copies of relevant legal and medical documentation. We agreed to prioritize transparency and clarity to aid efficient administrative review and decision-making. As a model, we referenced last year’s correspondence to ensure all established expectations are met.
