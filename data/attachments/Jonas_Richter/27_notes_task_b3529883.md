## Why I Chose These Items and Where I Look

Market day is a small ritual for me, and I plan my list around a mix of routine favorites and whatever looks especially inviting on the stalls.  
 
**Basil from Frau Lehmann**  
If Frau Lehmann is at her stall (she often is), getting her “surprise bundle” of basil is tradition for me. It’s reliably the freshest and often includes unusual varieties. If she’s not there, I’ll still find good basil at other herb sellers, but Frau Lehmann’s always feels special. Since basil wilts quickly, I use it right away—sometimes just torn into a salad on the same day.

**Fresh Produce Staples**  
Zucchini, potatoes, tomatoes, onions, garlic, and mixed greens are the backbone of my summer meals. I choose each item directly, checking for freshness and quality. These vegetables are easy to combine for salads, frittatas, simple sautés, and side dishes. I like knowing exactly where my food comes from, and it’s reassuring to buy straight from local growers.

**Bakery and Dairy Finds**  
Good bread is non-negotiable. Sironi’s sourdough has a satisfying chew and deep flavor, but the main bakery is also excellent for wholegrain loaves. Eggs from pasture-raised hens taste richer, and I’ve come to know a couple of the vendors—seeing familiar faces every week is part of what makes shopping here enjoyable. Cheese and quark are versatile; I use them with fruit and honey for breakfast or as a quick topping for baked potatoes.

**The Pantry Staples: Olive Oil and Nuts**  
I always need a good bottle of olive oil. The Mediterranean stalls carry options you can actually sample—a small ritual I look forward to. A handful of nuts or seeds in a salad keeps me fueled through busy days and brings welcome crunch.

**Seasonal Fruit and Variety**  
One of the pleasures of Markthalle Neun is seeing the changing offerings through the year. In July, juicy berries are irresistible, but if apples or stone fruit look best, I’ll adjust. I try to always bring home something vibrant and in season—I see it as a reward for eating fresh.

---

## How I Shop and Plan Quantities

I typically buy enough for myself, with a bit of flexibility in case I have visitors. The quantities listed are ideal for a week’s worth of meals and snacks—there’s rarely waste. If I’m planning to cook for friends or family, I just scale things up: more potatoes, an extra loaf, double the fruit. Keeping a list this clear makes trips smooth, even when the market is busy and bustling.

## Source Information

Markthalle Neun remains Berlin’s go-to for local produce and handmade goods. Most vendors are listed on their [official website](https://www.markthalleneun.de/), which I sometimes check the night before in case there are any special events or new stalls announced. Regular stops like Sironi for bread and Frau Lehmann for basil have become part of my shopping rhythm, but there’s always something unexpected to discover.
