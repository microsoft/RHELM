## Key Discussion Outcomes

### 1. Shared Objectives and Policy Brief Revision

Participants reached consensus on the need to update and reinforce the 'Privatbildung Jetzt!' policy brief. The focus will be on responding to current discussions shaping German education policy, with particular attention to practical implementation and the latest legislative developments. Emphasis will be placed on actionable, evidence-supported recommendations designed to facilitate stakeholder acceptance and guide policymakers.

### 2. Integration of Hamburg Case Study

Frau Becker highlighted the relevance of featuring Hamburg as a central case study. Hamburg demonstrates innovative approaches to governing private education, including regulatory mechanisms, efforts to increase access and diversity, and targeted improvements in educational outcomes.

It was agreed that including a detailed look at Hamburg would:
- Enhance the empirical grounding and credibility of the policy brief,
- Offer policymakers a validated example of private education reform in the German context,
- Provide concrete data for comparison with other federal states (Länder),
- Enable the identification of transferable lessons and best practices.

To ensure the Hamburg section is robust, recent data, relevant policy analyses, and peer-reviewed studies will be identified and referenced.

### 3. Next Steps and Deliverables

To support the policy brief revision, next steps were defined as follows:
- Complete an updated literature review focused on the outcomes and regulatory environment for private education in Hamburg,
- Develop a dedicated section summarizing Hamburg’s approach, implementation strategies, and results, combined with a discussion of broader implications,
- Revise the policy recommendations to incorporate insights drawn from Hamburg’s experience,
- Prepare a targeted stakeholder mapping identifying key actors (school leaders, parental organizations, education authorities) and mapping their respective interests as they relate to private education policy.

### 4. Task Assignment and Accountability

Responsibilities were allocated according to participants' areas of expertise. The group underscored the importance of systematic documentation and meeting agreed-upon deadlines. Each team member is also responsible for signalling any gaps in evidence or information dependencies as soon as they arise.
