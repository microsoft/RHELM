2. Event Materials, Devices and Contingency Checklist

Devices & Tech Essentials

Ensuring uninterrupted communication and data security is critical:

  • Fairphone 4 (Android 13): Fully secured, Signal for encrypted chats, personal device locked at all times.
  • HP EliteBook 845 G9 (Encrypted Laptop): All work presentations and sensitive documents are backed up both locally (Tresorit/Nextcloud) and offline. Full-disk encryption and latest OS patches, VPN (Mullvad/ProtonVPN) running.
  • Kingston IronKey D300S (USB-C encrypted key): Portable resource backup.
  • Jabra Evolve2 65 Headset: Reliable for both on-site calls and remote meetings, excellent noise cancellation.
  • Anker PowerCore 20100 mAh Power Bank: Keeps all devices charged, especially important during longer sessions away from outlets.

Printed & Digital Materials

  • Latest privacy curriculum: Ready as PDFs, PowerPoint, and ten color-printed handouts for in-person attendees.
  • Demo Device: Loaned Chromebook/tablet—factory reset, guest mode enabled for safe demonstrations.
  • Promotional flyers (A5, QR codes), stickers with campaign hashtag: For awareness building and to direct people to resource sites.
  • Personal credentials: Badge, lanyard (EU Digital Policy Association insignia).

Contingency Planning

No plan survives first contact with reality—so I always prepare backups:

  • Hard copies of all core slides and curriculum (ready to run paper-only session, if tech fails).
  • TP-Link M7350 MiFi Router: Reliable failover for connectivity.
  • Chargers/adapters (multi-country compatibility).
  • Notepad and pens: For jotting down observations or contact info the analog way.
  • Masks, sanitizer (in compliance with venue guidelines).
  • Weather gear: Business-casual, rain jacket and umbrella at the ready.

Digital Readiness

  • Files are saved in compatible formats (PowerPoint, LibreOffice, PDF), tested on demo hardware.
  • Follow-up email drafts for key contacts are pre-written and saved offline.
  • Emergency resource transfer USB ready.
