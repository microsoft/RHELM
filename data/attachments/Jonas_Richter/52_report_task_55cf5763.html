3. Campaign Brainstorm: Strategy and Focus

Goal:
Develop a high-impact, scalable campaign responding to current challenges and opportunities in European privacy, with a particular focus on developments expected at the end of 2024.

Key Issues and Strategic Approaches:

  • Engagement with the EU Artificial Intelligence Act (AI Act):
    We explored how to best respond to the imminent implementation of the EU AI Act, specifically its implications for personal privacy, algorithmic accountability, and biometric surveillance. We agreed on two fronts: first, we need to make the Act’s contents accessible to the general public, translating regulatory jargon into clear, relatable explanations; second, we plan to advocate for stronger privacy safeguards in high-risk contexts such as biometric data deployment, especially since enforcement guidelines remain unsettled.

  • GDPR Reform Advocacy:
    The group discussed recent GDPR amendment proposals before the European Parliament, with a focus on risks from new risk-based frameworks and calls to relax core privacy restrictions by both private and state actors. Our consensus was to center the campaign around defending and strengthening individual data protections in the face of industry and state pressure to dilute them, while highlighting how fundamental rights can be preserved even amid ongoing digital innovation.

  • German Data Surveillance Legislation:
    Several legislative proposals in the Bundestag, including expanded law enforcement access to telecommunications metadata and controversial “chat control” (mass scanning) initiatives, emerged as significant threats to civil liberties. We raised concerns about their chilling effects on democratic participation and activism. Our approach will combine rapid-response advocacy—such as open letters to lawmakers and strategic media outreach—with coalition-building across civil society and technologist communities.

Anticipated Impact:
By prioritizing public education, direct policymaker engagement, and alliance-building, we aim to influence legislative debate both at the EU and national levels, shift public narratives, and fortify the movement’s capacity to respond swiftly to new threats.
